Every unit is validated over 12,500 km of reliability testing before export, and Fenghan Trading configures battery, charging interface and options to your route, load and climate.

    FAQ — KTH3

    What is the battery warranty on the KTH3?

    CATL LFP packs carry an 8-year / 4,500-cycle warranty to 80% state of health, backed by Dongfeng and Fenghan Trading aftersales.

    Can the KTH3 be supplied right-hand drive?

    Factory RHD conversion is available on request (4–6 weeks additional lead time) — done at the plant, not aftermarket.

    What charger does the KTH3 need?

    The KTH3 charges via DC fast charge. We advise on depot charger selection and grid connection as part of every quotation.
