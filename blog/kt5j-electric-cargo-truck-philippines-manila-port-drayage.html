Manila Drayage Duty Cycle

A typical Manila drayage loop runs 15 to 40 km with heavy stop-start traffic and 30 to 90 minutes of gate queuing per trip. Diesel trucks in this class burn about 0.45 to 0.55 L/km under load, costing $0.40 to $0.72 per kilometer at Philippine diesel prices of $0.90 to $1.30 per liter, and the long idle burns fuel while earning nothing. The KT5J draws about 1.1 to 1.6 kWh/km when loaded, and at Philippine commercial electricity of $0.08 to $0.20 per kWh that drops to $0.09 to $0.32 per kilometer. Because the electric drivetrain draws no power at idle, the gate queue costs almost nothing in energy.

The KT5J carries a 210 kWh CATL LFP pack with an 8-year or 4,500-cycle warranty, and FOB pricing runs from $55,000 to $70,000, the lowest entry price in our export range. For a 40 km loop the 210 kWh pack delivers multiple round trips per charge, and the truck tops up between shifts at the depot.

Dual-Port Charging for Quick Turnaround

The KT5J supports dual-port charging, meaning two charge connectors can feed the pack at once to shorten the depot window. With two 180 kW ports the truck accepts up to 360 kW combined, moving from 10 to 80 percent in 45 to 90 minutes. For drayage fleets that run two shifts, this lets one charger serve several trucks across the day without a dedicated stall per unit. The dual-port design also gives redundancy: if one port is in use elsewhere, the truck still charges on the other.
