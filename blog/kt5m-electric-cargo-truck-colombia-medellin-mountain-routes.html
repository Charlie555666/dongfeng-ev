Regenerative Braking on 8 Percent Grades

On an 8 percent descent the KT5M's motor acts as a generator, charging the pack while holding a steady speed with little use of the friction brakes. This does two things at once: it extends range by recovering energy that a diesel truck wastes as heat, and it reduces brake wear, which is a major maintenance item on mountain routes. Drivers report steadier control because the single-speed drivetrain removes the need to hunt gears on a steep grade.
