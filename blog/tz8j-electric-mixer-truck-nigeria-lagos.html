Drum, Load, and Electric PTO

The TZ8J carries an 8 cubic meter drum rated for about 19,200 kilograms of wet concrete, driven by an electric power take-off fed from the same 420 kWh pack that drives the truck. Removing the separate diesel drum engine is a double win: no second fuel bill, no second exhaust on site, and drum speed held constant regardless of road speed, which protects mix quality on Lagos's stop-start roads.

In practice the drum load is modest next to traction. A 30 kilometer round trip with discharge uses roughly 18 to 22 percent of the pack, leaving comfortable margin for 8 to 12 deliveries per shift before a depot recharge.

Charging Plan at the Batching Plant

The TZ8J returns to the plant every shift, so a 240 kW DC charger at the depot restores 20 to 80 percent in about 75 minutes during loading windows and overnight. Plants running two shifts can add a second charger to keep four or five mixers rotating. Because the truck never leaves the plant's radius, battery swap is unnecessary.

  • Install one 240 kW charger per four mixers at the batching plant.
  • Charge during loading and discharge windows to avoid idle time.
  • Track energy per cubic meter delivered to benchmark each route.
  • Pre-cool the cab on grid power before the first morning run.
  • Keep one diesel mixer only for rare out-of-radius remote pours.

Cost per Cubic Meter Versus Diesel

A diesel mixer on a 30 kilometer Lagos round trip burns roughly 14 liters per load including idle time in traffic, about $11 at Nigerian diesel prices, plus a drum-engine fuel and service bill. The TZ8J uses about $2.50 of electricity per load at industrial tariffs, and removes the drum engine entirely. Across 10 loads a day that is roughly $90 saved per truck per day, or about $25,000 a year, before maintenance savings.

The FOB price of $110,000 to $150,000 is recovered within three to four years of continuous looping, and the 8-year or 4,500-cycle battery warranty covers the full typical asset life, so there is no mid-life battery replacement in the five-year budget model.
