Why Da Nang High-Rise Sites Favor an EV Mixer

Da Nang enforces nighttime noise limits that restrict diesel mixers near residential high-rises, and a ready-mix batch plant often runs overnight to hit a morning pour. The KT9X produces only tire and drum-pump noise because there is no engine, so a plant can schedule more night deliveries and still stay under the 75 dB threshold. That access to night windows is a real capacity gain that a diesel fleet partly loses to fines and complaints.

Short plant-to-site loops of 8 to 20 km mean the mixer returns to the batch plant charger every cycle, so one 360 kW DC unit serves four to six trucks across a shift. Regenerative braking on the loaded return from site to plant recovers 8 to 12 percent of the climb energy, lowering the cost per cubic meter poured.
