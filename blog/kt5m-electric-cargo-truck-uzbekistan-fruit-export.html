Why 336 kWh for Cold Chain

A reefer truck burns energy two ways: driving and cooling. The KT5M's 336 kWh pack is sized so the reefer and the road share one LFP source without starving either. CATL LFP chemistry supports the 8-year or 4,500-cycle battery warranty and tolerates the daily charge-discharge of a distribution fleet. The LvKong motor in the 282 kW class gives enough torque for fruit loads on regional grades, with smooth low-speed control that protects delicate cargo during yard moves.

Real-world range with a running reefer lands in the 200 to 350 km band depending on outside temperature and door openings. For the Tashkent-to-border leg that is usually sufficient within a single charge, with a packing-house top-up before departure to hold margin for traffic and inspection delays.

Tashkent Packing-House Charging

The natural charging point is the packing house, where trucks are loaded and held before departure. Downtime and Maintenance Savings

An electric reefer removes most of the failure points that idle a diesel unit on a cold-chain route. There is no engine oil to change, no diesel aftertreatment to clog, and far fewer rotating parts to wear in dust and heat. Regenerative braking on every stop recovers energy and cuts friction brake wear, which extends component life on a stop-start duty. For an exporter that measures availability by shipments per day, fewer unscheduled repairs mean more consistent cold chain and a smaller parts bill. The LFP pack's long cycle life also means the main energy component rarely needs attention inside the warranty window.
