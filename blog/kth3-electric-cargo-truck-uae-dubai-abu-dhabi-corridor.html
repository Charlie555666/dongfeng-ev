KTH3 Electric Cargo Truck on the Dubai-Abu Dhabi Corridor

The 130-150 km corridor between Dubai and Abu Dhabi - along E11 and E311 - is the UAE's densest freight lane, connecting Jebel Ali's port and free zone with the capital's industrial districts at ICAD and KEZAD. That distance is the sweet spot for a heavy electric truck: far enough to move real tonnage, short enough to run multiple daily rounds on one charge. The Corridor Duty Profile

Typical KTH3 work on the corridor runs full truckload distribution: Jebel Ali or DIP to Abu Dhabi industrial customers, returning with backhaul packaging, raw materials or empties. A round trip runs 260-300 km at highway speed - exactly one comfortable charge for the 400 kWh pack at 31-38 t gross weight, with a 15-20% reserve even in summer with full air conditioning. Fleets running two rounds per day add a 40-50 minute dual-gun DC charge during the midday loading window, converting driver waiting time into range.

Energy consumption at UAE highway speeds runs 1.3-1.5 kWh/km loaded, dropping toward 1.0-1.1 empty. The flat, straight corridor is gentle duty; the only real variable is summer heat, which the liquid-cooled pack handles with an 8-10% seasonal range adjustment.

Charging on E11 and E311

The corridor's charging plan is endpoint-based: fleet-owned chargers at the Dubai and Abu Dhabi depots, with the UAE's growing public truck-capable charging network as contingency. Dubai and Abu Dhabi have both moved fast on EV infrastructure, and several fuel-station operators now host high-power DC on the corridor itself. The operational rule: own your endpoints, use public chargers as backup, and schedule the midday charge into the loading dock window so it costs zero scheduled time.

  • Install 240 kW dual-gun units at both depots - one charger serves 3-4 trucks on staggered schedules.
  • Charge to 90% daily; reserve full charges for double-round days.
  • Register with the corridor's public charging networks before you need them.
  • Track kWh/km per driver; the spread between best and worst drivers on this flat corridor still reaches 12%.

Summer at 48 C

UAE summer is the harshest sustained-heat duty in the region: 45-48 C ambient, road surfaces above 65 C, and air conditioning running at full load all day. The KTH3's liquid thermal management holds the pack in its 20-35 C window and protects charging speed - the combination that defeats air-cooled trucks. Plan summer range at 300-320 km loaded rather than 340; schedule the heavier rounds for morning where possible; and keep tyre pressures checked weekly, because underinflation on hot highway surfaces costs range and casings.
