Why Karachi Mega Projects Favor an Electric Mixer Truck

Karachi's skyline is being rebuilt with large residential, port and infrastructure projects that demand reliable concrete delivery on tight pour windows. A diesel mixer idles its drum engine constantly to keep the mix turning, burning fuel even while parked at the site. The KT9X powers the drum electrically and can charge or maintain charge from grid power or a site genset, cutting the cost of every cubic meter delivered. With electricity at $0.08 to $0.20 per kWh versus diesel at $0.90 to $1.20 per liter, the per-kilometer and per-hour energy saving is large on a mixer that runs its drum most of the shift.
