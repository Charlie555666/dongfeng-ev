That predictability removes range risk entirely. The sealed LvKong motor also shrugs off the murram dust that clogs diesel air filters on Kenyan sites, cutting a weekly maintenance cost down to nearly zero. Payload, Gradeability, and Cycle Time

The TZ3V carries 25,000 kilograms of aggregate or muck and climbs a 30 percent grade fully loaded, covering the steep ramps found on dam benches and elevated road works. On a typical 15 kilometer site shuttle the truck completes 8 to 12 loaded cycles per shift, and regenerative braking on every loaded descent recovers 10 to 15 percent of the climb energy, stretching real-world range toward 200 kilometers even on hilly ground.

The electric drivetrain's instant torque also matters at the quarry face. A diesel tipper lugs and smokes when pulling away loaded on a grade; the TZ3V pulls cleanly from a standstill with full torque at zero rpm, which shortens cycle time and reduces clutch and driveline wear to effectively nil.

Charging Plan for Site Depots

Because the TZ3V returns to the contractor's yard each shift, a single 240 kW DC charger restores 20 to 80 percent in about 75 minutes during the midday break and overnight. Contractors running double shifts can pair one charger with three or four tippers on a staggered schedule. Kenya Power's industrial tariffs make overnight charging particularly cheap, and a solar canopy over the yard can cut the energy bill further.

  • Site a 240 kW charger at the contractor yard for break and overnight charging.
  • Stagger shift starts so one charger serves three to four trucks.
  • Train operators to maximize regen on loaded descents.
  • Track energy per ton hauled to benchmark each site loop.
  • Keep the charger canopy shaded to protect equipment from equatorial sun.

The battery warranty is the foundation of the ownership case: 8 years or 4,500 cycles on the CATL LFP pack, transferable to a second owner, which means the truck can be sold or refinanced mid-life with a factory guarantee still running. No diesel powertrain offers anything comparable, and that difference shows up in residual value, where documented, warranted electric trucks are clearing stronger percentages of original price than worn diesel equivalents.
