High-Pressure System Performance

The KT7A pairs an 8,000 liter baffled tank with a 16 MPa high-pressure pump feeding front spray bars, side lances, and a manual gun for street furniture and signage. Because the pump runs on electric power take-off, pressure stays constant regardless of road speed, so the truck can crawl through a crowded market street at 5 km/h while holding full washing pressure, something a diesel PTO tied to engine rpm cannot do smoothly.

The baffled stainless tank keeps the load stable on ramps and resists the brackish water many Saudi municipalities draw for street washing, extending tank life well beyond a mild-steel equivalent.

Range and Shift Planning in Extreme Heat

On night washing duty the KT7A returns 200 to 240 kilometers per charge and 5 to 7 hours of continuous washing, covering a full municipal shift. The liquid-cooled CATL LFP pack is rated for 45 °C-plus ambient operation, and night deployment keeps both the pack and the crew out of the worst heat. A 120 kW depot charger restores 20 to 80 percent in about 90 minutes between shifts.

  • Run washing shifts at night to protect pack life and crew safety.
  • Site a 120 kW charger at the municipal depot for inter-shift top-ups.
  • Use constant-pressure electric PTO for crowded pedestrian areas.
  • Pre-position water refill points along the night route.
  • Deploy extra units ahead of Hajj and Ramadan crowd peaks.
