Urban delivery is the lowest-risk electrification use case. Routes are short and predictable, vehicles return to the same depot every night, and stop-start traffic lets regenerative braking recover energy continuously. Hanoi's dense districts also mean a diesel truck's idle fuel burn and noise are pure waste, where the KT5M's electric drivetrain converts almost all its energy into movement. Payload and Body Options for City Freight

The KT5M carries 8,000 kilograms in a 32 cubic meter box, with flatbed, stake, and refrigerated variants available on the same chassis. For Hanoi's e-commerce and FMCG rounds, the box body with roller shutter or liftgate handles 80 to 120 drops per day. The low, flat floor made possible by the under-chassis battery pack maximizes usable cargo volume within the same overall length as a diesel equivalent.

The 282 kW LvKong motor gives brisk acceleration from traffic lights even fully loaded, which drivers notice immediately in Hanoi's dense traffic, and the single-pedal regen mode reduces fatigue across a long multi-drop shift.

Range and Charging for Urban Rounds

On the urban delivery cycle the KT5M returns 240 to 280 kilometers per charge, roughly double what a typical Hanoi delivery round consumes in a day. That margin means overnight depot charging alone is sufficient for most operators, with a 120 kW DC charger restoring 20 to 80 percent in about 90 minutes for the occasional double-shift day.

  • Charge overnight at the depot on off-peak tariffs.
  • Keep one 120 kW charger per four to five trucks for double-shift flexibility.
  • Use regen-heavy drive mode in dense districts to stretch range.
  • Plan multi-drop routes with telematics to cut empty kilometers.
  • Spec the refrigerated body variant for cold-chain rounds.

Warranty, Resale, and Lifecycle Confidence

The battery warranty is the foundation of the ownership case: 8 years or 4,500 cycles on the CATL LFP pack, transferable to a second owner, which means the truck can be sold or refinanced mid-life with a factory guarantee still running. No diesel powertrain offers anything comparable, and that difference shows up in residual value, where documented, warranted electric trucks are clearing stronger percentages of original price than worn diesel equivalents. Fleets that keep disciplined charging habits, staying between 20 and 90 percent state of charge in daily operation, routinely show 85 to 90 percent pack capacity after five years, and the telematics health report converts that directly into resale price.
