Range and Payload for Regional Hauls

The KT5L carries a 10,000 kilogram payload at a gross combination weight of 18,000 kilograms, and returns 260 to 300 kilometers of range per charge when loaded on rolling regional roads. That covers the typical Khouribga-to-Casablanca leg with margin, and the 300 kWh CATL LFP pack holds charge well in Morocco's warm but not extreme climate. For multi-drop port distribution, the same range supports a full day of shorter loops without a midday charge.

The LvKong motor's 360 kW peak output gives the KT5L confident overtaking and grade performance on the inland highways, and regenerative braking recovers energy on the descending return from the plateau to the coast. Drivers report that the smooth electric torque also reduces cargo shift damage on bagged phosphate products.

Charging Plan for the Logistics Chain

Because the KT5L returns to a depot or port yard each shift, the charging plan is simple: a 120 kW DC charger at the dispatch yard restores 20 to 80 percent in about 90 minutes during the break and overnight. Logistics operators running continuous shifts can add a second charger to keep a fleet of ten or more trucks rotating. Battery swap is unnecessary because every leg starts and ends at a known charging point.

  • Install a 120 kW charger at each plant dispatch yard and the port gate.
  • Stagger departure times so chargers serve multiple trucks per shift.
  • Use telematics to schedule charges during off-peak grid hours.
  • Keep one diesel tractor only for the rare long out-of-radius leg.
  • Train drivers on eco mode to stretch range on the inland climbs.
