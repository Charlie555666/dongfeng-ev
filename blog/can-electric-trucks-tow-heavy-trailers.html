What Hitch, Brake and Safety Rules Apply?

Electric tractors tow on standard hardware under standard rules - fifth-wheel couplings, electrical and pneumatic connections, and trailer brake systems identical to diesel practice. Regenerative braking integrates with trailer brakes through the same pneumatic control drivers already know, with the electric system handling the first stage of retardation and friction blending seamlessly behind it - tractor-trailer brake balance is factory-calibrated and workshop-adjustable. Driver training adds one towing-specific habit: anticipating regeneration behavior with a heavy trailer behind, which most drivers master within days. Weight distribution and fifth-wheel positioning follow standard practice. Nothing about the towing interface is exotic - which is exactly the point fleets should want to hear.

Should You Tow with Electric or Keep a Diesel for Trailer Work?

Match the tool to the duty cycle. Long-haul beyond daily range currently favors planning: corridor charging is expanding, but routes must be mapped honestly.
