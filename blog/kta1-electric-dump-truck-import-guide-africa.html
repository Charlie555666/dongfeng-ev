Step 1 - Confirm homologation

Before you pay a deposit, confirm the Dongfeng KTA1 meets Ghana HV safety (UN R100), braking and lighting rules. Dongfeng supplies the type-approval dossier; your clearing agent maps it to local standards. Allow 4-10 weeks for first-time West African bauxite and road construction registration, and start this clock before production so the truck is not parked awaiting paper.

Step 2 - Proforma invoice and contract

The proforma invoice states the Dongfeng KTA1, VIN range, $85,000-$110,000 FOB value, Incoterm and lead time. Use FOB (free on board) so you control freight and insurance, or CIF if you prefer the exporter to deliver to Ghana port. Lock the spec sheet, body type and colour in writing to avoid a mismatch between the built EV truck and the import licence.

Step 3 - Shipping and Incoterms

EV trucks ship roll-on/roll-off or in flat-rack containers; HV batteries are declared as UN3480 Class 9 with the correct MSDS and pack test summary. Book freight early in peak season and insure to invoice value. Because the LFP pack is below the air-freight limit for many carriers, sea freight is the norm and keeps landed cost predictable for fleet buyers.

Step 4 - Customs clearance

  • Bill of Lading and commercial invoice (value matches proforma).
  • Packing list and HS classification for the electric truck.
  • UN3480 battery declaration and MSDS / test summary.
  • Certificate of Origin for preferential duty where applicable.
  • Type-approval / homologation acceptance from Ghana authorities.

Step 5 - Lead times and insurance

Typical production lead time for the Dongfeng KTA1 is 30-60 days after deposit, plus 25-45 days sea transit to Ghana. Insure to 110% of invoice, and confirm the policy covers HV batteries as declared goods. Budget a week of buffer for West African bauxite and road construction port congestion at peak.

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to import an electric truck?

You need eight core documents: proforma invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, export license, certificate of conformity, plus the UN38.3 test summary and MSDS covering the 262 kWh LFP battery. Fenghan Trading supplies all eight with the shipment.

How long does shipping take to African ports?

Sea freight runs 25-40 days: about 30 days to East African ports such as Mombasa and Dar es Salaam, and 35-40 days to West African ports such as Lagos, Tema and Abidjan. Add 5-10 days for customs clearance.

What is the FOB price of the KTA1?

The KTA1 electric dump truck FOB price is $95,000-$125,000 depending on battery capacity and body options. The standard 262 kWh CATL LFP configuration with 220 km real-world range sits near the middle of that band.

Do EV trucks pay lower import duty in Africa?

In many African markets, yes - several countries apply 0-10% duty on battery-electric trucks versus 25-35% on diesel equivalents. Confirm the current EV concession schedule with your clearing agent before ordering.

How do I charge the KTA1 at my site?

A 120 kW DC depot charger takes the 262 kWh pack from 20 to 80 percent in about 80 minutes; a full overnight charge covers a full shift. Where swap stations exist, a battery swap takes 5-6 minutes.

Planning depot charging for this EV truck

Charging an electric truck is a depot discipline, not a public-network gamble. A 262 kWh pack working a 220 km day typically returns with 25 to 35 percent in reserve, so overnight demand is about 157 kWh per truck. On a 120 kW DC charger the pack climbs from 20 to 80 percent in roughly 79 minutes; on 240 kW that falls to about 39 minutes, which lets two trucks share one charger on staggered shifts.

Size the site transformer for your busiest shift plus 30 percent headroom, keep cable runs under 30 metres to limit voltage drop, and meter each charger separately so energy cost per truck stays visible. In Ghana, grid connection lead times of three to nine months are common, so apply for the connection the day you sign the truck order, not the day the ship arrives.
