KT7A Electric Washing Truck for UAE Municipal Fleets

UAE municipalities run some of the most demanding street-cleaning programs in the world - boulevards, corniches and industrial roads washed nightly to keep the city image spotless through dust season and summer. That work is quiet-hours, short-loop, depot-based duty: a perfect fit for an electric truck. The Dongfeng KT7A electric washing truck combines a 232 kWh CATL LFP pack with a 308 kW drivetrain on a 25 t chassis, and this guide covers the municipal case for UAE fleets.

The Municipal Washing Cycle

A UAE municipal washer works the night shift: 20:00 to 06:00, circulating fixed routes of 80-140 km with high-pressure pumps running most of that time. The duty is crawl-speed - 8-20 km/h while washing - with constant pump load. A diesel washer burns 18-25 litres per hour in this service, engine at fast idle to drive the PTO pump. The KT7A drives its pumps electrically at exactly the power the nozzles demand, and measured shift consumption runs 55-70% of the pack for a full 9-10 hour night, including transit to and from the depot.

Water capacity and refill logistics shape the shift more than energy: a full tank covers 4-6 km of road washing at municipal rates, so routes are planned around hydrant or tanker refill points - a pattern unchanged from diesel, and one the telematics system logs automatically for the municipality's service reports.

The Noise Case for Night Work

Municipal washing happens at night specifically to avoid traffic - which puts it directly beside sleeping residential districts. Diesel washers at 80-85 dB generate steady complaint volumes; the KT7A at working speed is barely louder than the water hitting the road. Municipalities in Abu Dhabi and Dubai increasingly write noise ceilings into cleaning contracts, and an electric fleet meets them without schedule compromises. The worker-exposure benefit matters too: crews walking beside the truck with hand lances work in clean air instead of diesel exhaust for a full shift.

  • Charge during the day at the municipal depot; the night-shift schedule makes energy windows easy.
  • Map refill points into route planning software; water logistics, not battery, set the shift rhythm.
  • Use the quiet operation deliberately - schedule the densest residential zones for the post-midnight window.
  • Log water and kWh per km washed; both metrics feed the municipality's sustainability reporting.

Summer Heat and Dust Season

UAE summer night work still means 38-45 C ambient with high humidity on the coast. The KT7A's liquid-cooled battery and motor hold performance through the night shift with a modest 6-8% seasonal range adjustment. Shamal dust season is when the fleet earns its keep hardest - washing demand spikes exactly when airborne particulate peaks - and the high-capacity filtration on the truck's cooling intakes handles it with a weekly filter service interval. The sealed IP67 high-voltage system is indifferent to the constant water exposure that washing duty involves.

Municipal Economics

The financial case runs through fuel and maintenance: a diesel washer on nightly UAE municipal duty burns $25,000-35,000 of fuel per year; the KT7A's electricity for the same service costs $6,000-9,000. The pump-drive efficiency and deleted engine maintenance halve the service budget. UAE government procurement increasingly weights emissions and noise in tender evaluation - documented on our UAE electric truck market page - so the electric fleet strengthens the bid as well as the budget. Payback against a diesel washer lands around year three on operating savings.
