Cold-Start Performance

Winter is the real test for any EV truck in Kazakhstan. The TZ3V's pack heating brings cells to operating temperature on grid power before the shift, so the first haul leaves with full available range. LFP retains a usable fraction of capacity at -20°C, and the liquid loop keeps the pack in its efficient window even when ambient drops far lower.

  • Pre-heating on grid power avoids wasting traction energy warming the pack.
  • LFP chemistry tolerates deep cold better than many alternatives.
  • Regenerative braking on loaded descent returns energy on every cycle.
  • Cabin heat draws from the main pack, not a separate diesel burner.
  • Centralized monitoring protects the 8-year / 4,500-cycle warranty in hard duty.

Cost Per Tonne Hauled

Mining fleets price everything per tonne moved.
