Why a Compact EV Cargo Truck Fits Santo Domingo

The KT5J electric cargo truck is the right electric truck for Santo Domingo's dense last-mile network, where narrow colonial streets and mall-delivery windows punish oversized diesel vans. With a 282 kWh CATL LFP pack and a 260 kW motor it returns 220 to 270 km of real range, enough for a full day of 12 to 18 drops without a mid-shift charge.

Dominican fleets value predictable cost and clean operation for supermarket and pharmacy contracts that now specify low-emission delivery. The KT5J's silent running also lets it serve residential zones early without noise complaints, extending the productive day.

Charging and Depot Setup

Most Dominican distributors already have three-phase power, so a 240 kW DC charger is a simple install. Fenghan can specify a dual-gun unit so two KT5J trucks share a shift, and battery swap is not offered so the schedule is the control variable.

Body Options and Payload

The KT5J takes a 5.5 to 6.8 m dry box, a refrigerated box for pharmacy and food, or an open flatbed for white goods, so a single platform serves supermarket, medical, and appliance distributors across Santo Domingo. The 282 kWh pack holds 8 to 10 t GVW in all bodies, and the 260 kW motor handles the colonial-centre hills without strain.

Insurers in the Dominican Republic increasingly discount electric commercial fleets because the sealed battery removes the fire and spill risk of a diesel tank, and the telematics give a verified speed and geofence record after any incident. That lower premium is a small but real line in the operating budget that favors the KT5J over an older diesel van.
