Hub Charging Layout

The sorting hub is the natural charging home - trucks return between waves, and the hub's power service already supports industrial loads. The working layout for a 20-truck fleet: overnight AC or low-power DC posts for the base fleet across the 22:00-05:00 window, plus two 120 kW DC units for the midday peak-season top-ups. Vietnamese industrial tariffs keep overnight energy cost low, and smart charging that schedules against the tariff curve saves a further 10-15% on the energy line.

  • Assign every truck a numbered bay; peak season punishes shared-post improvisation.
  • Charge to 90% daily; the festival-peak weeks get the full-charge schedule.
  • Meter kWh per route wave; exceptions flag dock-delay problems as much as truck problems.
  • Book the midday DC window into the sorting schedule - charging aligned to operations costs no time.
