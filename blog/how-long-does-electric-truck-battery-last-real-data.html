How Many Cycles Does an LFP Electric Truck Battery Deliver?

Lithium iron phosphate is the dominant chemistry in Chinese heavy electric trucks for one reason: cycle life. A CATL LFP cell rated for commercial vehicle duty is tested to 4,500 full equivalent cycles at 100% depth of discharge before its state of health drops to 80%. NCM cells of the same size typically reach 2,000-2,500 cycles. In daily fleet language, one cycle equals one full discharge and recharge, so a truck that charges once per working day consumes about 300 cycles per year.

Run that arithmetic on a Dongfeng TE8L electric tractor with the 466 kWh pack: 4,500 cycles divided by 300 cycles per year gives 15 years of theoretical cycle life. In practice calendar aging and thermal stress shorten that, which is why the warranty lands at 8 years. Even at that conservative figure, the battery outlasts the first ownership period of most fleets in Africa, the Middle East and Latin America.

What Degrades an Electric Truck Battery in Hot Climates?

Three factors control degradation: temperature, state of charge at rest, and charge rate. Heat is the one buyers in the Gulf, North Africa and Southeast Asia ask about most. Sustained cell temperatures above 45 C accelerate side reactions inside the cell; sustained storage above 80% SOC at high temperature multiplies the effect. Fast charging above 1C adds stress, but LFP tolerates it far better than NCM.

Dongfeng electric trucks counter this with liquid thermal management on every export pack. The coolant loop holds cells between 20 C and 35 C even when ambient air hits 50 C in the Saudi Arabia electric truck market, which is the single biggest reason fleet data from Riyadh and Doha shows only 1.8-2.3% capacity loss per year instead of the 4-5% seen on passively cooled vans. Parking discipline matters too: fleets that leave trucks at 40-60% SOC overnight instead of 100% measurably slow calendar aging.

What Buyers Should Check Before Ordering

  • Ask for the cell datasheet: insist on CATL LFP with a rated cycle count of 4,500 or more, not generic Chinese cells.
  • Confirm liquid cooling is standard on your export spec, not an option deleted to cut price.
  • Get the warranty in writing with the SOH threshold stated (70% is the export standard).
  • Plan depot software that caps daily charging at 90% SOC unless the route truly needs the full pack.
  • Record a baseline capacity test at delivery so degradation claims are easy to document later.

How many years does an electric truck battery last in daily fleet use?

A CATL LFP electric truck battery lasts 8-10 years in daily fleet use, rated at 4,500 full cycles to 80% capacity, and Dongfeng export packs carry an 8-year or 450,000 km warranty to 70% state of health.

Does fast charging shorten electric truck battery life?

Fast charging at 120-240 kW has a minimal effect on LFP packs with liquid cooling; fleet data shows roughly 2% annual capacity loss whether trucks charge at 120 kW or 240 kW, provided cell temperature stays under 35 C.
