Battery Health: The Core of Used EV Valuation

In a used electric truck, the battery report is the valuation. A 400 kWh CATL LFP pack showing 85-90% state of health after four or five years still delivers most of its original 240 km range, and LFP chemistry degrades slowly and predictably enough that the remaining life is bankable. Buyers should demand the BMS health report, charging history and cycle count, and should understand what they read: daily fast-charging history is worth a discount versus gentle overnight charging, and any gaps in the record are a red flag. The 8-year or 4,500-cycle battery warranty transfers with the truck, so a three-year-old unit still carries five years of factory coverage - a floor under value that no used diesel can offer.

Inspection Checklist Before You Buy

A used electric truck inspection covers familiar ground plus EV-specific items. The conventional checks - chassis, suspension, brakes, tires, accident repair evidence - follow standard practice. The electric layer adds: the battery health report and its consistency with the odometer; a full diagnostic scan for stored high-voltage fault codes; charging inlet and cable condition; a live fast-charge test confirming the pack accepts rated power; and verification that BMS software is current. Cosmetics matter less than records. A KT9X with complete telematics and service history is worth a premium over a shinier truck with missing files. We support buyers with pre-purchase inspection guidance and can arrange factory-side verification for units originally supplied through us.

Import Process and Documents for Myanmar

Importing a used electric truck into Myanmar follows the same skeleton as any used commercial vehicle import, with battery-specific additions. The file includes the purchase invoice, deregistration or export documents from the origin country, bill of lading, and the conformity paperwork Myanmar requires - pre-shipment inspection where applicable. The lithium battery adds shipping documentation: the UN38.3 test summary and a dangerous goods declaration, with state of charge limited to around 30% for ocean transit. Used vehicle age or inspection rules in Myanmar must be checked before purchase, not after - some markets restrict commercial vehicle imports by age. Clearance at Yangon Port runs smoothly when the document pack is complete and pre-lodged with a licensed broker in Yangon.

Refurbishment Budget and Parts

Used electric truck refurbishment is cheaper than diesel refurbishment because there is no engine to overhaul. A realistic preparation budget of $1,500-$3,500 covers cosmetics, brake service, tires where needed, cabin consumables, a full high-voltage inspection and BMS software update. Battery module replacement, rarely needed on LFP packs with good health reports, is the one large-ticket possibility - which is why the health report matters so much at purchase. Parts supply for the KT9X runs through our Xi'an warehouse with air freight for urgent items, and the parts catalog with factory numbers ships with every unit.
