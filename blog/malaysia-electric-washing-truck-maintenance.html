EV vs Diesel Maintenance: What Disappears

The maintenance case for electric trucks starts with subtraction. Gone are engine oil and filter changes, fuel filters, injectors, turbochargers, DPF regeneration, exhaust aftertreatment fluid, clutch wear on automated manuals and most transmission servicing. What remains is a shorter list: coolant for the battery and motor thermal loop, brake fluid, gear oil in the drive axle, cabin filters, tires, and friction brakes that last two to three times longer thanks to regenerative braking. For a fleet in Malaysia, this typically cuts scheduled maintenance hours per truck by 50-60% and parts spend by 40% or more. The KT7A concentrates its service needs into predictable, clean, workshop-friendly tasks.

Battery Care in Malaysian Climate

Climate shapes battery care more than any other factor. In hot Malaysian conditions, the rules are simple: use the thermal management system as designed, avoid parking at 100% charge in extreme heat for days, and favor overnight or shaded charging when ambient temperatures peak. The liquid-cooled CATL LFP pack in the KT7A manages cell temperatures actively, so normal operation needs no heroics - just respect for the charging windows in the operator manual. Fleets that follow these basics consistently see degradation at the gentle end of the warranted curve. Cold-climate operation reverses the advice: precondition the pack while plugged in, and expect a modest winter range reduction that planning should absorb.

Maintenance Cost per Kilometer
