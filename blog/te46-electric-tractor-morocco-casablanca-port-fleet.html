Diesel yard tractors in this service idle away 30-45% of engine hours in crane queues. Charging Strategy Inside the Port

Port charging succeeds or fails on siting. The layout that works at Casablanca: two 180-240 kW DC chargers at the tractor parking yard, positioned on the natural return path from the crane lines, plus one unit near the ro-ro ramp if that operation sits far from the main yard. Tractors plug in during the 30-45 minute shift handover; a 20-80% top-up in that window restores a full shift of energy. Moroccan industrial tariffs keep the energy cost per container move near one-third of the diesel figure.

  • Assign chargers by berth zone so tractors never cross the yard empty to plug in.
  • Stagger shift changes by 15 minutes across teams to avoid charger queuing.
  • Use telematics SOC alarms at 25% - terminal tractors should never deep-discharge in queue lines.
  • Keep charging bays under canopy; Casablanca's salt air accelerates connector corrosion.
