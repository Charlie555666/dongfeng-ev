KTH3 Electric Cargo Truck for Nigeria Pharma Cold Chain

The KTH3 electric cargo truck is an electric truck built for Nigeria's pharma cold chain, and the KTH3 heavy electric cargo truck carries a 424 kWh CATL LFP battery to hold reefer temperature on long Lagos-Abuja runs. Pharmaceutical logistics is unforgiving about temperature, and the EV argument here is not only about emissions but about removing the diesel heat source from next to the cargo.

Why Pharma Cold Chain Needs an EV Truck

Nigerian vaccines, insulin and biologics move under strict 2-8 °C control, and any temperature break spoils stock worth far more than the fuel to move it. Diesel reefers idle the cooling unit constantly and add heat in the cargo bay, working against the very temperature they are meant to hold. An electric truck runs the reefer from the main pack and the drivetrain from the same energy source, with no tailpipe heat near the load. In the Nigeria electric truck market, cold-chain is the EV segment with the clearest cargo-protection case because the failure mode of a broken cold chain is a total loss, not a delay.

There is also a traceability story. An electric reefer with a logged state of charge and temperature stream is easier to certify than a diesel unit with a separate, poorly monitored cooling set, which matters for donors and regulators auditing a vaccine programme.
