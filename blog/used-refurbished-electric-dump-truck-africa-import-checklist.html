Used and Refurbished Electric Dump Trucks: An African Importer's Checklist

Buying a used or refurbished electric truck can lower the entry price for an African importer, but the battery decides whether the saving is real, and the new KTA1 electric dump truck remains the benchmark every second-hand unit must beat. This checklist walks Nigerian and wider African buyers through state-of-health testing, refurbished pricing bands, inspection steps, and the point at which a new KTA1 wins.

Why importers consider used or refurbished EV trucks

A new KTA1 6x4 dump carries a FOB price of $95,000 to $125,000 with a fresh 350 kWh CATL LFP pack and a full 8-year / 4,500-cycle warranty. A refurbished unit can list 30-50% lower, which is attractive for a first African deployment or a tight municipal budget. The catch is the pack: a battery at 70% state-of-health still drives, but its range and residual value are sharply lower, so the purchase price must be discounted accordingly rather than taken at face value.

Battery state-of-health test procedure

  • Request the pack's onboard SOH report and cross-check it with an independent capacity test.
  • Charge to 100% and discharge at a known load to measure real kWh versus the 350 kWh nameplate.
  • Inspect cell-voltage balance; a spread over 50 mV signals ageing modules.
  • Verify the thermal system holds temperature under a 45 °C ambient load test.
  • Confirm the high-voltage interlock and insulation monitor still pass fault tests.

Only accept a unit whose measured capacity matches the seller's SOH claim within 3-5%. A 350 kWh pack at 80% SOH effectively delivers about 280 kWh of usable energy, cutting the 230-280 km range toward the low end and shortening the 8-year horizon.

Physical and electrical inspection checklist

Beyond the pack, inspect the 400 kW motor for bearing noise, the tipper hydraulics for seal weep, the brake system for regen wear patterns, and the charging port for arc damage from repeated DC sessions. Check the chassis for quarry or coastal corrosion, especially if the unit came from a humid or saline environment. Request the full service log; a gap longer than the 8-year / 4,500-cycle window suggests the pack was cycled hard without records.

When a new KTA1 wins

The new KTA1 electric dump truck wins whenever the refurbished discount is under 25% or the pack SOH is below 80%. The full 8-year / 4,500-cycle warranty, the fresh 350 kWh CATL LFP pack, and the 360 kW charging standard give a predictable cost that a refurbished unit with unknown history cannot match. For a first African deployment where downtime is costly, the new KTA1's support network usually pays back the premium within the first contract.

Total cost of ownership: new versus refurbished

Over five years a new KTA1 at $95,000-$125,000 with a full 8-year / 4,500-cycle warranty typically costs less in risk than a refurbished unit at 40% discount but with a pack at 75% SOH. The new 350 kWh pack needs no service, while the refurbished one may need a $20,000-$30,000 module swap inside year two, erasing the upfront saving. At 230-280 km range and $0.11/kWh, the new truck's energy and maintenance advantage adds roughly $9,000 a year, so the premium pays back inside the first contract for a daily-duty operator.

Spare parts and support for imported units

Whether new or refurbished, the 400 kW motor, the CATL LFP pack contactors, and the 240-360 kW charger modules share the same Dongfeng part numbers, so a Lagos service point can support both. For a refurbished unit, insist the seller transfers the remaining warranty or supplies a pack-service certificate, and keep one spare charge-port and one spare contactor on the shelf so a fault never strands a tipper beyond a day.

Documents to verify before import

  • Independent SOH and capacity test report on the pack.
  • Full service log covering the prior duty cycle.
  • Customs and duty assessment for used EV truck import.
  • Residual-value and warranty-transfer schedule.

Verify these in writing before payment; a missing SOH report is a reason to walk away.

Import and registration into Nigeria

Size the national opportunity in the Nigeria electric truck market before committing, then route the unit through Lagos or another bonded terminal. A used EV truck still needs a customs broker, duty assessment, and commercial registration; budget for a pre-clearance inspection so the SOH claim is verified on home soil. Plan a 240 kW or 360 kW charger at the operator's depot regardless of new or refurbished, because both draw the same CATL LFP charging standard.

Refurbished versus new: a worked example

Take a unit offered at $60,000 against a new KTA1 at $110,000, a 45% discount that looks attractive until the pack test shows 74% SOH. At 350 kWh nameplate that is only 259 kWh usable, cutting range to roughly 170-200 km and signalling a pack service of $22,000 inside two years. The all-in cost becomes $82,000 plus downtime risk, erasing most of the saving versus a new truck whose 350 kWh pack and 8-year warranty need nothing. The lesson: price the discount against measured SOH, not the sticker.

Warranty transfer and pack service centres

For any refurbished unit, insist the remaining CATL LFP warranty transfers to the African importer in writing, or obtain a pack-service certificate from an authorised centre. Lagos and other hubs can service the 400 kW motor and contactors, but a pack module swap needs the correct cells and a calibrated balancer, so confirm the centre before purchase. A transferable 8-year / 4,500-cycle cover is worth more than a further 5% off the price.

When a refurbished unit is the right call

A refurbished KTA1 makes sense only when the pack SOH is above 85%, the warranty transfers, and the duty cycle is light, such as a single-shift municipal tipper. For daily heavy hauling, the new KTA1's predictable cost and support network win. Match the choice to load count and km, and let the SOH report, not the discount, decide.

Resale and end-of-life battery value

Even a KTA1 pack at 80% SOH retains value as stationary storage once the truck retires, so the battery is not a write-off at end of life. Buyers should factor this residual into the refurbished-versus-new maths, because a new pack's second life offsets part of its premium. Document the cycle count from day one so the pack's storage value is provable to the next owner, keeping the Dongfeng EV truck asset liquid in the African market.

Next steps for importers

Request a written SOH report and an independent capacity test on any refurbished unit, and price the pack service before you negotiate. Compare the all-in cost against a new KTA1 with its full warranty, and only proceed with used stock when the discount clearly covers the battery risk. With a verified pack and a planned charger, an African importer can deploy electric dumping capacity confidently, new or refurbished.
