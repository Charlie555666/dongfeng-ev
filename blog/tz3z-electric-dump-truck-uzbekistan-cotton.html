Shuttle Performance on Earthworks

On a typical 5 to 15 kilometer canal or leveling loop the TZ3Z completes 10 to 14 loaded cycles per shift, and regenerative braking on every loaded descent returns 10 to 15 percent of the cycle energy to the pack. The instant electric torque pulls the 22-ton payload out of soft borrow-pit ground without the clutch slip and driveline shock a diesel suffers, which shortens cycle time and cuts wear on unpaved farm tracks.

Charging From a Cheap Grid

Uzbek industrial electricity tariffs are low enough that the energy cost per cycle is almost a rounding error next to diesel. A 240 kW charger at the field base restores 20 to 80 percent in about 75 minutes during breaks and overnight, and the seasonal schedule means the charger can travel between project bases on a trailer as the work moves.

  • Move the charger between seasonal project bases by trailer.

Warranty, Resale, and Lifecycle Confidence

The battery warranty is the foundation of the ownership case: 8 years or 4,500 cycles on the CATL LFP pack, transferable to a second owner, which means the truck can be sold or refinanced mid-life with a factory guarantee still running. No diesel powertrain offers anything comparable, and that difference shows up in residual value, where documented, warranted electric trucks are clearing stronger percentages of original price than worn diesel equivalents.
