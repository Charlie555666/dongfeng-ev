Why Tires Deserve Their Own Budget Line on Electric Trucks

Tires are the second-largest consumable cost on any truck fleet after energy, and on electric trucks they deserve fresh attention rather than inherited diesel habits. Two electric-specific factors change the wear equation: instant torque, which stresses tread at every launch if drivers treat the throttle like a diesel, and battery mass, which raises axle loads on the steer and drive positions. Neither is a problem when managed - fleets running electric heavy-haul tractor duty report tire life comparable to diesel once drivers are trained and pressures are disciplined - but unmanaged, both quietly add 15-25% to tire cost per kilometer. The TE9L electric heavy-haul tractor pairs its 510 kW motor with a torque delivery that telematics can meter precisely, turning tire wear from a surprise into a measured variable.

What TPMS Actually Buys a Fleet

Tire pressure monitoring systems pay for themselves on any truck; on electric trucks they pay faster, because pressure discipline protects both tires and range. A tire running 15% under specification increases rolling resistance enough to cost measurable kilometers of range per charge - range the fleet paid for in battery capacity. TPMS on the TE9L reports per-position pressure and temperature continuously, alerting before slow leaks become roadside stops and before heat buildup signals overload or brake drag. For Qatari fleets, the practical gains are threefold: fewer roadside tire events, tread life stretched toward its engineered limit, and energy consumption held at the figure the range planning assumed. Specify TPMS at order time; retrofitting is possible but factory integration is cleaner and cheaper.

Electric Truck Tire Wear Patterns and What They Mean

Electric trucks wear tires differently, and reading the patterns tells you about driving and loading before it tells you about the tires. Shoulder wear on steer axles points to under-inflation or aggressive cornering; center wear points to over-inflation; rapid drive-axle tread loss with cupping points to harsh launch habits that a torque-limited eco mode cures in a day. The TE9L's telematics complements the visual inspection: launch current and acceleration data correlate directly with drive-tire wear rates, so fleet managers can coach the two drivers who burn tires instead of retraining all forty. On Qatari road surfaces - laterite, broken asphalt, concrete - alignment and rotation intervals matter more than on European highways; build local road reality into the maintenance calendar.

Rotation, Matching and Position Strategy

A disciplined rotation program is the cheapest tire life extension available. The standard pattern on a 6x4 or 8x4 heavy-haul tractor: rotate steer tires to trailer or trailing positions before shoulder wear passes the halfway mark, match duals by diameter within tolerance, and never mix tread depths across a drive axle, which forces the differential to work continuously. Electric trucks add one wrinkle: regenerative braking loads the drive axle differently under deceleration, which makes drive-position alignment checks worth shortening. Fleets in Qatar running the TE9L on rotation schedules of 25,000-35,000 km, adjusted for route severity, report drive-tire life at or above diesel benchmarks - and casing quality good enough for retreading, which halves the per-kilometer cost again on positions where retreads are permitted.

Tire Budgeting: Cost per Kilometer Done Honestly

Budget tires per kilometer, not per year, and the electric comparison stays honest. A heavy heavy-haul tractor tire position costs $400-$900 depending on brand and market; a full set replacement on the TE9L is a four-figure event that lands on schedule when wear is managed, and at random when it is not. Model three scenarios: disciplined TPMS plus rotation, baseline diesel habits carried over, and neglected maintenance - the spread between them is typically 20-30% of total tire spend, which on a high-mileage fleet is real money. Include the range effect: under-inflated tires tax every charge.
