What Duties Apply to an Electric Truck in Indonesia?

Indonesia applies import duty on completely built-up trucks under HS 8704, with electric trucks currently benefiting from a lower most-favored-nation rate than diesel equivalents under the green vehicle incentive framework. On top of duty, buyers pay VAT at 11 percent and a luxury goods tax that varies by gross vehicle weight and battery capacity. The 600 kWh CATL LFP pack in the TZ3V pushes energy density rather than engine size, which keeps the luxury tax band lower than a comparable diesel dump.

Buyers should request a landed-cost breakdown that separates freight, insurance and the Indonesian charges so the true cost of the electric truck is visible.
