Bahrain's Manama municipality is upgrading its waterfront and boulevard cleaning, and city fleets want street washers that do not add diesel fumes to the streets they clean, which makes a 282 kWh electric washing truck a practical municipal upgrade. The KT7A electric washing truck carries a CATL LFP battery and an LvKong motor sized for steady pump and chassis duty, delivering an EV truck that covers long dawn cleaning routes on a single charge.

Why Manama street washing fits electric

Manama runs washing trucks on 40-70 km loops through the waterfront, souq districts and embassy boulevards, mostly at low speed before the morning traffic builds. Those quiet, stop-frequent routes are ideal for battery-electric trucks: the pump load is steady, regenerative braking recovers energy at every turn, and the silent chassis lets crews work at dawn without disturbing residents. A diesel washer on this duty burns 0.45-0.50 L/km, and at Bahraini diesel near $0.95/L that is about $0.45/km. The KT7A cuts that to roughly $0.15/km at municipal electricity of $0.09/kWh and 1.2-1.4 kWh/km.

Because the truck often idles the pump while parked at a plaza, a diesel unit wastes fuel merely running the engine to drive the washer. The KT7A drives the pump from the high-voltage pack, so the chassis stays silent at the kerb while cleaning proceeds.

Maintenance savings: what 35-40% really means

An electric washing truck removes the diesel engine's oil, filters, injectors, turbo, EGR and exhaust after-treatment - the parts that drive most unscheduled breakdowns on low-speed municipal routes. Our fleet data shows EV trucks cut scheduled maintenance by 35-40% versus a diesel equivalent, which on a washer running 200 days a year is roughly $2,600 in saved workshop bills. The LvKong motor's sealed drivetrain also avoids the pump-bearing wear that shortens diesel washer life in Gulf heat.

  • No engine oil changes: save about $800 per year in oil and filters.
  • No injector or turbo service: save about $700 per year.
  • Brake wear cut by regenerative braking at turns: save about $600 per year.
  • Longer tyre life from smooth electric torque: save about $500 per year.
