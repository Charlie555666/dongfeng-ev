TE8M Electric Tractor for Jordan Phosphate Haulage: Aqaba Route

Why Jordan Phosphate Hauls Are Moving to the EV Truck

A heavy EV truck is the natural fit for Jordan's phosphate corridor because the route is fixed, the tonnage is constant, and the port of Aqaba sets a predictable destination. The TE8M electric tractor is a 6x4 prime mover built for exactly this work: a 420 kWh CATL LFP pack, LvKong motors up to 510 kW, and the torque curve that a 60-ton gross combination weight demands on the climb out of the rift valley. For a fleet running 120 km each way between mine and port, the electric tractor turns a fuel headache into a managed energy plan.

Phosphate is Jordan's largest export by volume, and the haulage contract is won or lost on cost per tonne-kilometer. Diesel tractors in this class burn 38 to 45 liters per 100 km under a loaded trailer, which at Jordan diesel near 0.90 USD per liter is a heavy line item. The TE8M attacks that line with regen on the empty return and off-peak charging at the depot.

Route Profile and Gross Combination Weight

The Aqaba corridor runs from the central phosphate mines near Al Hasa and Al Abiad down to the port, a distance of roughly 110 to 140 km depending on the loading point. The outbound leg is loaded at a GCW near 60 ton, the return is empty near 18 ton. That asymmetry is perfect for an EV truck because the descent toward Aqaba recovers energy that the climb on the empty return barely uses.

Operators plan two round trips per shift where the duty allows, with a mid-day top-up at the port or mine charger. The table below shows a representative single-trip energy plan for the TE8M on the phosphate run.

Torque, Motors and Climb Performance

The TE8M uses a LvKong permanent-magnet motor rated up to 510 kW with peak torque near 2,400 Nm at the wheel. That output is available from a standstill, which matters on the graded climbs of the rift valley where a diesel tractor must downshift and spool up. The electric tractor simply holds the grade at constant speed, and the driver reports less fatigue over a 10-hour shift.

  • Constant torque from 0 rpm removes the need for 12-speed manual shifting on the grade.
  • Regen on the descent holds speed without riding the brakes, cutting lining wear.
  • Single-speed reduction lowers transmission failure risk versus a 16-speed diesel box.
  • Quiet cab improves communication on the port apron and reduces driver fatigue.

Because the motor is sealed and liquid-cooled, the only thermal task on the climb is pack temperature, which the TE8M holds under 42°C even in a Jordan summer of 42°C ambient. Heat is a manageable variable, not a limit.

Charging Plan for the Mining Corridor

The depot charging plan is the heart of the business case. A 240 kW DC unit charged on the Jordan night tariff near 0.06 USD per kWh fills the 420 kWh pack for about 25 USD. The same diesel tractor burns 110 liters per round trip at 0.90 USD, or 99 USD. The energy saving alone is 74 USD per trip, and a two-trip daily shift saves 148 USD per tractor per day.

Fleets add a 500 kWh stationary buffer so the charger never draws peak grid during the day, which protects against demand charges and keeps the site inside its contracted power. Where land allows, 300 kW of carport PV covers the buffer top-up and trims the bill further during long summer days.

Operator Training and Port Interface

Aqaba port has its own safety and emissions rules, and the TE8M helps operators comply because it produces no tailpipe smoke on the apron. Drivers complete a half-day high-voltage awareness session plus a port-specific induction, and the depot keeps a written emergency plan for any pack event. The quiet operation also reduces conflict with port pedestrians and crane crews.

Relay scheduling is the final piece: stagger three tractors so one is always on the road, one is loading, and one is charging. That cadence keeps the 240 kW charger busy but never overloaded, and it lifts asset utilization above what a single diesel tractor achieves on the same shift pattern.

Many phosphate fleets already own land near the mine gate, so the depot is a low-cost add-on rather than a fresh build.
