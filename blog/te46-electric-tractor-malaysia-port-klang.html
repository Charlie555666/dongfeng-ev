Why Port Klang Yard Work Favors an EV

Port Klang runs some of the highest container volumes in Southeast Asia, and yard tractors operate in tight loops of 1 to 4 km that return to a central swap or charge hub every cycle. A diesel yard tractor idles up to 60 percent of its shift, burning fuel and filling the terminal with NOx; the TE46 instead swaps a pack in 6 minutes or fast-charges during the mandatory rest, eliminating idle emissions entirely. For an operator running 30 tractors, that is a measurable cut in the terminal's carbon intensity report.

Battery swap is the differentiator at a 24-hour terminal: instead of parking a tractor for an hour to charge, the driver rolls through a swap station and is back on the block in 6 minutes.
