Warranty Scope in Extreme Heat

The TE8P is delivered with a comprehensive warranty built around the realities of Gulf operation. The high-voltage battery pack is covered for 8 years or 4,500 charge cycles, whichever comes first, on a capacity-retention basis of 70% state of health. The LvKong drive motor rated at 420 kW is covered for 5 years or 300,000 km. The vehicle control unit, high-voltage cabling, and thermal management hardware carry a 3-year or 200,000 km coverage. Critically, the warranty is written for high-temperature markets: it remains valid when the truck operates in sustained 50°C ambient conditions, provided the recommended cooling-fluid service intervals are observed at the prescribed odometer marks.

What sets the TE8P apart from generic imported electric trucks is that heat derating is treated as a covered design parameter rather than an exclusion. If the battery management system fails to maintain a safe operating temperature under documented Gulf duty cycles, the remedy is covered. Operators should retain service records, because proof of cooling-system maintenance is a condition of heat-related claims. The export team supplies a bilingual Arabic and English service logbook template so that local workshops record the correct data points in a form the warranty desk accepts.

Beyond the headline terms, the warranty also covers the reduction in available range caused by sustained heat, provided the operator follows the published summer duty limit. Many cheaper imported electric trucks explicitly exclude heat-related capacity loss, leaving the operator to absorb the cost silently. The TE8P warranty instead treats the Gulf duty cycle as the design baseline, which is precisely why it is the preferred electric truck for Saudi fleets that cannot tolerate hidden exclusions buried in fine print.

Battery Thermal Management Coverage

The TE8P uses liquid-cooled LFP architecture from CATL, with a 424 kWh pack and active thermal management tuned for hot climates. The warranty explicitly covers the chiller, plate-cooling loop, coolant pump, and battery management system sensors. Thermal management is the single most important subsystem in Saudi operation, because every degree of pack temperature above the optimum band accelerates calendar aging and reduces cycle life. Coverage includes firmware updates that optimize charge curves for summer, delivered over the air or at the depot during a scheduled visit.
