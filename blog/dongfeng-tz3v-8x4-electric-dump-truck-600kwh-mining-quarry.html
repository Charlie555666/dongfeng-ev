Dongfeng TZ3V 8x4 Electric Dump Truck: 600 kWh CATL Power for Mining and Heavy Haulage

The Dongfeng TZ3V 8x4 pure electric dump truck is the flagship heavy-haul configuration in our export line-up, purpose-built for open-pit mining, quarry operations, and large-scale infrastructure projects where diesel fuel costs, ventilation, and ESG targets are pushing operators toward zero-emission solutions. With a CATL 600 kWh lithium-iron-phosphate (LFP) battery pack, dual 400/550 kW permanent-magnet motors, and a structural gross combination weight rating of 95 tonnes, the TZ3V is engineered to replace the diesel 8x4 workhorse in the most demanding operating environments of Africa, the Middle East, and Central Asia.

CATL 600 kWh LFP Battery Pack

The TZ3V traction battery is a 618.24 V nominal CATL LFP pack with 600 kWh of usable energy. LFP chemistry was selected over NMC for three reasons that matter on a mine site or in tropical export markets:

  • Thermal stability. LFP cathodes do not undergo oxygen release at high state-of-charge, eliminating thermal runaway propagation between cells. The pack has passed GB 38031 and UN 38.3 abuse testing including nail penetration, overcharge, external short circuit, and 130 °C hot-box exposure.
  • Cycle life. LFP cells routinely exceed 4,000 full equivalent cycles at 80 percent depth-of-discharge, equivalent to more than 10 years of typical fleet operation. The TZ3V is warranted for 5 years or 300,000 km (whichever comes first) on the battery system for off-road / construction use, with extension options to 500,000 km available.
  • Temperature tolerance. The pack operates from -30 °C to +55 °C ambient with a liquid thermal management loop that pre-conditions cells during DC fast charging and active mining work cycles.

Operators report real-world loaded range of 180 to 250 km on a full charge, depending on payload, gradient, ambient temperature, and auxiliary load. For a typical 30-tonne payload quarry cycle of 15 to 20 km loaded plus 15 to 20 km empty return on grade, the truck completes one and sometimes two full shifts per charge before requiring a top-up.

Dual 400/550 kW Motor Drivetrain

Power is delivered through a TZ460XS permanent-magnet synchronous motor rated at 400 kW continuous and 550 kW peak, with peak torque of 2,800 Nm at the motor shaft. A heavy-duty 4-speed automated manual transmission (Fastgear / Shanxi-derived, designation J4S280DD) transfers torque to the tandem rear axles through a single-reduction hypoid gear set. Top speed is governed at 85 km/h, and gradeability at GCW is 30 percent.

Regenerative braking is standard and returns up to 0.35 g of deceleration on a fully loaded downhill descent, recovering energy back into the battery and reducing service-brake wear. For mine operators running loaded downhill descents every shift, this feature cuts brake-pad replacement intervals by 50 to 70 percent compared with conventional diesel 8x4 dumpers.

Structural Capacity and Body Configuration

The TZ3V is rated for a gross combination weight of 95 tonnes and a structural body payload of up to 60 tonnes. Standard body dimensions are 7,200 mm x 2,300 mm x 1,300 mm (length x width x side height) giving approximately 21.5 cubic metres of struck capacity, with options for 1,500 mm high sides for coal and 1,100 mm for dense iron ore. Body steel is HB400 wear plate on the floor and HB300 on the sides, and a HYVA FC-series front-end tipping cylinder provides 50-degree dump angle within 30 seconds.

The chassis uses a 850 + 850 mm parallel ladder frame with 10 mm + 5 mm double-layer reinforcement at the front and mid sections, engineered to absorb the twisting loads of half-loaded operation on uneven mine benches. The front axle is rated 9 tonnes and the tandem rear axles are 2 x 16 tonnes, with hub reduction and optional diff locks for low-traction surfaces.

Operating Economics vs Diesel 8x4

In markets where diesel fuel is imported and subject to foreign-currency volatility (much of West Africa, Central Asia, and the Sahel), the energy cost per kilometre of the TZ3V is typically 55 to 70 percent lower than a comparable diesel 8x4 dumper when charged from grid or captive solar. The maintenance cost is also lower because there is no engine oil, no diesel particulate filter, no AdBlue, and no transmission fluid change-out beyond a single 80,000 km service interval.
