Why Basra Port Logistics Need a Heavy Electric Cargo Truck

Basra handles the bulk of Iraq's maritime trade, and cargo moves from the terminals through the city to distribution yards and onward trucks. Diesel trucks burn fuel while queuing at gates and idling in traffic, a double penalty in a hot climate where the engine fan and air conditioning load stay high all day. The KTH3's electric drivetrain produces peak torque from zero rpm, so fully loaded starts from port ramps are clean and quick, and there is no idling fuel burn while drivers wait for customs clearance.

For Iraqi fleet owners, the 25 t GVW class covers the majority of port drayage and yard transfers without needing a tractor trailer combination. A single KTH3 can shuttle containers from quay to inland depot and return on a single charge when the route is planned, which is exactly the duty cycle Basra operators run.
