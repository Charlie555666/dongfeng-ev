Why the EEC Corridor Fits an Electric Shuttle

The EEC is a tight cluster of plants 10 to 40 km apart, so a shuttle truck returns to a central plant charger every few hours and one DC unit serves a whole intra-corridor fleet. A diesel shuttle idles at every gate and emissions bay; the KT5J holds air conditioning and lighting on stored energy and drives the next leg silently. For a Rayong campus moving 80 inbound cages per day, the consistency of electric range removes the diesel price swings that have hit Thai logistics margins.

Thai BOI promotes green industry, and several EEC campuses now grant preferred parking and lower utility demand charges to electric logistics fleets. An electric shuttle with telematics data gives your plant a verified decarbonization figure that supports both BOI reporting and the carbon targets of the Japanese and Western OEMs that anchor the corridor.
