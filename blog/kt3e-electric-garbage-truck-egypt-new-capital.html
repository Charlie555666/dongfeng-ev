Depot Charging and Swap

Most municipalities charge at the depot overnight, when power is cheapest. For two-shift cities, a 5 to 6 minute battery swap keeps trucks moving without a charging window. Fleet managers should model total depot load with the utility before scaling unit count so the night charge does not strain the local network, and should stage chargers to avoid a single overloaded feeder.

Downtime and Maintenance Savings

An electric garbage truck removes most of the failure points that idle a diesel unit on a collection route. There is no engine oil to change, no diesel aftertreatment to clog, and far fewer rotating parts to wear in dust and heat. For a municipality that measures availability by routes per day, fewer unscheduled repairs mean more consistent collection and a smaller parts bill.
