Range and Thermal Management at 45 Degrees

Desert heat is the first objection every Saudi fleet manager raises, and it is exactly what the TE8P's engineering answers. The CATL LFP chemistry tolerates high ambient temperature far better than NMC, and the liquid cooling loop holds cell temperature in the safe band through midday summer operation. In practice the pack delivers 280 to 330 kilometers of loaded highway range in summer, rising toward 350 kilometers in the mild winter months when cabin and battery cooling loads fall.

Regenerative braking adds real value on the long grade descents approaching the coastal plain. Drivers report recovering 8 to 12 percent of outbound energy on a round trip when they use a disciplined regen strategy, which is effectively free range on every loaded return.

Charging Plan for Saudi Trunk Routes

The TE8P supports 240 kW DC fast charging, restoring 20 to 80 percent in about 75 minutes, and an optional battery-swap specification that exchanges the pack in 5 to 6 minutes for fleets running continuous shifts. The practical Saudi model is a depot charger at each end of the corridor plus one mid-route opportunity charger at a logistics park, which turns a 400 kilometer leg into a comfortable one-charge operation.

  • Install 240 kW chargers at the Riyadh and Dammam depots for overnight and break top-ups.
  • Add one 360 kW opportunity charger at the mid-corridor logistics park.
  • Pre-cool the cab on grid power before departure to save traction energy.
  • Schedule the heaviest loads on the cooler morning window in summer.
  • Choose the swap variant if your trailers run two shifts without a 75-minute gap.
