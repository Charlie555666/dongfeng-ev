Why Hanoi Collection Rounds Suit Electric

Refuse collection is stop-start work at walking pace, the single best duty cycle for regenerative braking, and the worst possible use of a diesel engine, which idles and smokes its way through the round. Hanoi's narrow residential streets and night collection windows amplify the noise advantage: the KT3E works at a fraction of a diesel's noise level, which cuts complaints and lets districts extend collection hours. Compactor and Bin-Lift Operation

The KT3E's 14 cubic meter compactor with a 4-to-1 ratio halves the tip runs a district round requires, which directly cuts the kilometers and energy per tonne collected. The electric PTO drives the compactor hydraulics without a second engine, so the whole body works silently at the kerb, and crews report lower fatigue on night rounds because there is no diesel vibration or exhaust at the loading position.

The bin lift services 120, 240, and 1,100 liter containers, covering Hanoi's mixed residential and commercial stock without modification, and the smooth electric torque reduces spillage on the district's steep alley ramps.

Range and Depot Charging

A loaded Hanoi collection round returns 200 to 240 kilometers per charge, comfortably covering a district loop with margin, and the 350 kWh pack recharges from 20 to 80 percent in about 90 minutes on a 240 kW depot charger. Because the trucks return to the depot every shift, overnight charging alone covers daily operation, with fast charging reserved for double-shift peaks during holiday waste surges.

  • Charge overnight at the depot on off-peak tariffs.
  • Use the 4-to-1 compaction to halve transfer-station trips.
  • Standardize container types to speed the bin-lift cycle.
  • Train crews on regen to recover energy at every stop.
  • Reserve fast charging for holiday peak double shifts.

Warranty, Resale, and Lifecycle Confidence

The battery warranty is the foundation of the ownership case: 8 years or 4,500 cycles on the CATL LFP pack, transferable to a second owner, which means the truck can be sold or refinanced mid-life with a factory guarantee still running. No diesel powertrain offers anything comparable, and that difference shows up in residual value, where documented, warranted electric trucks are clearing stronger percentages of original price than worn diesel equivalents.
