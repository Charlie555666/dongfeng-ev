Dust Suppression and Filtration

The KT1D mists ahead of its brushes from the 1,200 liter tank, lifts fines into a sealed 4,000 liter hopper, and filters expelled air through a two-stage cyclone plus panel system, so it captures Riyadh's fine dust rather than re-suspending it. Operators report 40 to 60 lane-kilometers per 6 to 8 hour night shift depending on debris load, comfortably covering an arterial district per unit per night.

Night Operation and Heat Management

Night deployment is doubly smart in Riyadh: it avoids the daytime heat that stresses any battery, and the near-silent electric drivetrain lets sweepers work residential districts and entertainment zones without complaints. The liquid-cooled CATL LFP pack holds its thermal band through Gulf summer nights, and a 120 kW depot charger restores 20 to 80 percent in about 90 minutes between shifts.

  • Run pre-dawn shifts to protect the pack from peak heat.
  • Mist continuously during shamal dust season.
  • One 120 kW depot charger serves three to four sweepers.
  • Schedule hopper dumps at fixed points on the night route.
  • Log lane-kilometers for municipal performance reporting.
