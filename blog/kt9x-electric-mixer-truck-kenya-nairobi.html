Drum and Electric PTO in Traffic

The KT9X's electric PTO holds drum speed constant regardless of road speed, so mix quality survives Nairobi's worst jams, and there is no separate diesel drum engine adding fuel, noise, and exhaust at the pour. A 30-kilometer round trip with discharge uses roughly 18 to 22 percent of the 420 kWh pack, supporting 8 to 12 deliveries per shift before a depot recharge.

Charging at the Batching Plant

The mixer returns to the plant every shift, so a 240 kW charger at the depot restores 20 to 80 percent in about 75 minutes during loading windows and overnight. Plants running two shifts add a second charger and keep four or five mixers rotating without touching the pour schedule.

  • Install one 240 kW charger per four mixers at the plant.
  • Charge during loading and discharge windows.
  • Track energy per cubic meter delivered by route.
  • Use overnight off-peak tariffs for the bulk of charging.
  • Keep one diesel mixer only for out-of-radius remote pours.

Warranty, Resale, and Lifecycle Confidence

The battery warranty is the foundation of the ownership case: 8 years or 4,500 cycles on the CATL LFP pack, transferable to a second owner, which means the truck can be sold or refinanced mid-life with a factory guarantee still running. No diesel powertrain offers anything comparable, and that difference shows up in residual value, where documented, warranted electric trucks are clearing stronger percentages of original price than worn diesel equivalents. Fleets that keep disciplined charging habits, staying between 20 and 90 percent state of charge in daily operation, routinely show 85 to 90 percent pack capacity after five years, and the telematics health report converts that directly into resale price.

Maintenance economics reinforce the point. The sealed LvKong motor has roughly one-third the moving parts of a diesel powertrain, with no oil, injectors, turbocharger, clutch, or exhaust aftertreatment to service, and regenerative braking cuts friction brake wear by an estimated 40 percent.
