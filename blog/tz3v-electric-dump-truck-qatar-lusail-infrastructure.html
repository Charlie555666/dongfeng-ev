Why Lusail earthworks suit a 600 kWh tipper

Most Lusail infrastructure hauls run on 5-15 km site loops between excavation pits, fill zones and crushing plants. Those micro-routes are perfect for battery-electric dump trucks: short, repeatable, and never far from a yard charger. Diesel tippers on this duty burn 0.50-0.55 L/km under 30-tonne load, and at Qatari diesel near $0.95/L that is about $0.50/km. The TZ3V drops that to roughly $0.22/km at site electricity of $0.11/kWh and 1.6-1.8 kWh/km loaded.

Because the loops are short, the TZ3V's 600 kWh pack delivers a full shift of 40-60 km of loaded running between charges, and regenerative braking on the descent back to the pit recovers 15-20% of the energy spent climbing out.

Maintenance savings: what 35-40% really means

An electric dump truck removes the diesel engine's oil, filters, injectors, turbo, EGR and exhaust after-treatment - the parts that drive most unscheduled breakdowns on dusty Lusail sites. Our fleet data shows EV trucks cut scheduled maintenance by 35-40% versus a diesel equivalent, which on a tipper running 250 shifts a year is roughly $5,500 in saved workshop bills. The LvKong motor's sealed drivetrain also resists the fine Gulf dust that clogs diesel air filters, so site reliability improves rather than degrades in heat.

  • No engine oil changes: save about $1,600 per year in oil and filters.
  • No injector or turbo service: save about $1,400 per year.
  • Brake wear cut by regenerative braking on pit descents: save about $1,300 per year.
  • Longer tyre life from smooth electric torque: save about $1,200 per year.
