Why Dar es Salaam needs a quiet EV truck compactor

The coastal capital runs a narrow collection window. Low-income wards sit close to the road, and early-morning diesel engines draw steady complaints about noise and exhaust fumes. An electric truck removes both problems at once. The KT3E drive train produces almost no audible gear noise at low speed, so crews can run the first sweep from 05:00 without disturbing residents who sleep beside the route. Exhaust and particulate emissions drop to zero at the curb, which matters in a city where informal sorting and recycling happen right next to the vehicle. Quieter operation also lets municipalities extend collection into evening hours without noise permits, raising daily throughput per truck.

Full-shift range and duty cycle

A typical Dar route covers 90-140 km per shift with repeated stop-start compaction and long idle periods at each collection point. At 1.4-1.6 kWh per km loaded, the 282 kWh pack delivers roughly 200-250 km of real range, enough for two full collection loops before a midday top-up. The liquid-cooled pack holds its capacity in the 25-35 C coastal climate without thermal derating, and the 8-year warranty covers the full municipal service life of the vehicle. Regenerative braking recovers energy on the rolling return trips, which extends effective range on hilly wards such as those on the Msasani peninsula.

Depot charging plan for municipal fleets

  • Install two 120 kW DC chargers at the depot and charge overnight on off-peak tariffs.
  • Top up to 80 percent between the morning and afternoon shifts in 60-90 minutes.
  • Set the charge window to 00:00-05:00 to capture the cheapest grid hours.
  • Keep one diesel unit as a reserve during the first six weeks of transition.
  • Train two operators per shift on the charger touchscreen and the battery dashboard.

Climate and battery durability on the coast

The Indian Ocean humidity and salt spray are hard on any vehicle, but the KT3E battery enclosure is sealed to IP67 and the liquid cooling loop keeps cells inside the 25-35 C operating window even when ambient heat climbs. The CATL LFP chemistry is inherently stable and tolerant of high cycle counts, which is why the pack carries an 8-year or 4,500-cycle warranty. Fleet managers in Dar es Salaam report that the absence of a hot diesel engine bay also lowers cabin temperatures for the crew during the hottest months of the year.

Driver training and safety on electric trucks

Moving from a manual diesel compactor to an electric truck changes the driver routine. There is no clutch, no gearshift and almost no engine braking feel, so new operators need a half-day session on smooth acceleration and on using regeneration to hold speed on descents. The KT3E keeps a 12 V auxiliary battery for lights and the compactor hydraulics, and the high-voltage system isolates automatically in a fault. Our training pack includes a printed checklist in English and Swahili so that every shift starts with a clean safety walk-around.
