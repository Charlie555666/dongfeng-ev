Reefer Power Draw and Range Math

A refrigerated electric truck must budget energy for both motion and cooling. At an ambient of 35 deg C, a well-insulated box needs roughly 6 to 10 kW to hold minus 18 deg C, which subtracts about 30 percent from the dry-range figure. Operators plan loops accordingly and use a 240 kW DC charger for a fast midday top-up when a route exceeds 180 km.

  • Spec a 350 kWh pack minimum for any route over 150 km with active cooling.
  • Pre-cool the box on shore power before departure to save battery.
  • Use 240 kW DC charging at the consolidation hub for 70-minute recovery.
  • Monitor reefer draw through telematics to catch a failing compressor early.
  • Keep the insulated panel thickness at 80 mm for tropical ambient.
