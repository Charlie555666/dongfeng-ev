What the 8-Year Battery Warranty Covers

The battery is half the value of an electric truck, so its warranty defines the real risk of ownership. The TZ5Y carries a CATL LFP pack of 600 kWh covered for 8 years or 4,500 full-equivalent cycles, with a guaranteed minimum capacity retention - typically 70-80% state of health - at the end of the term. Coverage includes cell defects, BMS failures and capacity loss beyond the guaranteed threshold under documented operating conditions. Normal gradual degradation within the guaranteed band is not a defect; the warranty promises the pack stays above the floor. Claims are settled by repair or module replacement, which restores the truck to service without replacing the entire pack.

Drivetrain and Chassis Coverage Terms

Beyond the battery, the electric drivetrain carries its own coverage: the 510 kW LvKong motor, motor controller, onboard charger and high-voltage distribution components typically carry multi-year terms, while the chassis, axles, suspension and cab follow conventional commercial vehicle warranty practice. Wear items - brake pads, tires, wiper blades, filters - sit outside warranty as they do on diesel trucks, though regenerative braking stretches friction brake life dramatically on an electric 80t mining truck. The critical discipline for fleet owners is service record keeping: warranty terms require scheduled inspections at documented intervals, and the telematics log on the TZ5Y makes that documentation automatic.

How to File a Claim from Peru

A warranty claim from Peru follows a defined path: the fleet reports the fault with the VIN, telematics data and photos or video of the symptom; our technical team triages remotely and either resolves it by guidance or authorizes local repair; parts ship from stock with freight terms defined in the warranty statement. Battery claims add the BMS health log, which the TZ5Y records continuously - this data-first process settles claims faster than traditional strip-down diagnosis. Most claims resolve in days for guidance-level issues and two to four weeks where parts must ship to Lima. The key to speed is complete first-time reporting with the data the factory needs.

Warranty Exclusions to Watch

Every warranty has boundaries, and knowing them prevents disputes. Standard exclusions cover accident damage, unauthorized modification of high-voltage systems, operation outside published load and gradient limits, water ingress beyond rated wading depth, and use of non-compliant charging equipment that damages the pack. Battery capacity claims require operation within the documented temperature and charging windows - data the BMS logs automatically. Fleets should also note that third-party repairs on high-voltage systems without factory authorization can void related coverage. None of these exclusions are unusual; they mirror diesel practice. The difference is that an electric truck's telemetry makes compliance provable, which protects honest operators in any dispute.
