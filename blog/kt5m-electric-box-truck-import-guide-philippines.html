Step 1 - Confirm homologation

Before you pay a deposit, confirm the Dongfeng KT5M meets Philippines HV safety (UN R100), braking and lighting rules. Dongfeng supplies the type-approval dossier; your clearing agent maps it to local standards. Allow 4-10 weeks for first-time island distribution and retail replenishment registration, and start this clock before production so the truck is not parked awaiting paper.

Step 2 - Proforma invoice and contract

The proforma invoice states the Dongfeng KT5M, VIN range, $50,000-$68,000 FOB value, Incoterm and lead time. Use FOB (free on board) so you control freight and insurance, or CIF if you prefer the exporter to deliver to Philippines port. Lock the spec sheet, body type and colour in writing to avoid a mismatch between the built EV truck and the import licence.

Step 3 - Shipping and Incoterms

EV trucks ship roll-on/roll-off or in flat-rack containers; HV batteries are declared as UN3480 Class 9 with the correct MSDS and pack test summary. Book freight early in peak season and insure to invoice value. Because the LFP pack is below the air-freight limit for many carriers, sea freight is the norm and keeps landed cost predictable for fleet buyers.

Step 4 - Customs clearance

  • Bill of Lading and commercial invoice (value matches proforma).
  • Packing list and HS classification for the electric truck.
  • UN3480 battery declaration and MSDS / test summary.
  • Certificate of Origin for preferential duty where applicable.
  • Type-approval / homologation acceptance from Philippines authorities.

Step 5 - Lead times and insurance

Typical production lead time for the Dongfeng KT5M is 30-60 days after deposit, plus 25-45 days sea transit to Philippines. Insure to 110% of invoice, and confirm the policy covers HV batteries as declared goods. Budget a week of buffer for island distribution and retail replenishment port congestion at peak.

Planning depot charging for this EV truck

Charging an electric truck is a depot discipline, not a public-network gamble. A 140 kWh pack working a 280 km day typically returns with 25 to 35 percent in reserve, so overnight demand is about 84 kWh per truck. On a 120 kW DC charger the pack climbs from 20 to 80 percent in roughly 42 minutes; on 240 kW that falls to about 21 minutes, which lets two trucks share one charger on staggered shifts.

Size the site transformer for your busiest shift plus 30 percent headroom, keep cable runs under 30 metres to limit voltage drop, and meter each charger separately so energy cost per truck stays visible. In Philippines, grid connection lead times of three to nine months are common, so apply for the connection the day you sign the truck order, not the day the ship arrives. Shaanxi Fenghan Trading includes a charger layout drawing and load calculation with every EV truck quotation, so your electrician can price the civil works before the truck lands.

Energy cost per kilometre: EV truck versus diesel

A comparable diesel box truck burns about 0.38 litres per kilometre in mixed duty; at $0.95 per litre that is roughly $0.36 per km. The electric equivalent consumes about 1.15 kWh per km fully loaded; at an industrial tariff of $0.10 per kWh the cost is near $0.12 per km, and overnight or solar-coupled charging can push it below $0.08. Over 70,000 km a year the saving is about $17,000 per truck, before any carbon or low-emission-zone benefit.

The gap widens in stop-start duty, where regenerative braking returns 15 to 25 percent of traction energy instead of burning it as brake dust. In Philippines, where diesel quality and price both fluctuate, locking in an electricity tariff also removes a major budgeting variable from the fleet P&L.

Maintenance and uptime economics

The KT5M has roughly one moving drivetrain part where a diesel has hundreds: no engine oil, no DPF, no turbocharger, no clutch, no multi-gear transmission service. Scheduled maintenance lands near $0.035 per km against $0.11 per km for a diesel equivalent, a saving of more than $5,000 a year at typical utilisation. Brake pads last two to three times longer because the motor does most of the deceleration.

Uptime matters more than parts cost. Fleet data from Chinese operators running the same e-axle platform shows 96 to 98 percent availability once drivers are trained, versus 92 to 94 percent for diesel trucks of the same age. Keep a small stock of filters, coolant, brake pads and 12V batteries on site; Shaanxi Fenghan Trading quotes a two-year consumables kit with every truck so the first service interval never waits on freight.

What to confirm before you order

  • Duty cycle: longest daily route, loaded and empty legs, gradient profile, and ambient temperature range.
  • Charging: available transformer capacity, overnight window, and whether opportunity charging or battery swap fits the shift plan.
  • Homologation: which national or regional standard applies in Philippines, and who holds the type-approval paperwork.
  • Payload: body weight versus rated GVW, so the 280 km real range holds with your actual load.
  • Support: local partner for HV work, spare-parts stock level, and response time commitments in writing.
  • Finance: FOB or CFR terms, letter-of-credit conditions, and currency cover for the balance on arrival.
