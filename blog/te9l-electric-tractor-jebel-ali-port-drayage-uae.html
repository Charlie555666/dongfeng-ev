Jebel Ali Drayage Is the Ideal EV Tractor Duty Cycle

Port drayage is a repeatable, short-radius loop: container terminal to inland depot to terminal, almost always inside a 50 km radius, at low average speed, with heavy standing loads. That is precisely where a battery-electric tractor beats diesel, because regenerative braking on every terminal approach recovers energy and the truck returns to a fixed depot every shift for predictable charging.

The UAE, and Dubai in particular, has paired this duty cycle with clean-energy targets and free-zone incentives that make the total cost of ownership favorable even before fuel savings are counted. Terminal operators also value the elimination of diesel fumes inside the yard, where thousands of workers and stacked containers trap combustion exhaust.

How the 600 kWh Pack Covers a Full Shift

A typical Jebel Ali drayage shift is 10-12 hours with 6-10 container moves. At an average consumption of around 1.6-1.8 kWh per km under load, the 600 kWh pack delivers the 300-350 km practical range the spec promises, and the liquid-cooled CATL LFP chemistry holds that performance through Gulf summers.

  • Each terminal approach recovers 5-15% of the trip energy through regenerative braking.
  • Depot charging at 240 kW or 360 kW returns 20-80% in 45-90 minutes during meal and rest breaks.
  • The 8-year / 4,500-cycle warranty matches a typical drayage asset life.
  • Idle air-conditioning draws far less than a diesel unit's hotel load because there is no engine to cool.
  • A single overnight charge plus one midday top-up covers the busiest shifts.

Charging Infrastructure at the Terminal

Terminal operators planning a TE9L fleet should size the depot for opportunity charging rather than full overnight fills. Because the trucks return every few hours, a bank of 240 kW DC units keeps the fleet rolling without a massive substation. The TE9L electric tractor accepts the full 120 / 240 / 360 kW range, so the same yard can host smaller and larger units.

The 45-90 minute 20-80% window is short enough that a shared charger rotates across several tractors during the natural breaks in a drayage day, which keeps capital cost per truck low and avoids over-building electrical capacity at the gate.

EV Truck Economics for UAE Ports

The FOB band of $135,000 to $180,000 sits alongside premium diesel tractors, but the operating gap is wide. Diesel tractors on drayage duty burn heavily at low speed and idle constantly; the electric unit's energy cost per km is a fraction of that, and maintenance is simpler with no exhaust, DPF, or complex transmission. For a fleet running 200+ km per day, payback often lands inside three years.

When Dubai's clean-corridor incentives and the avoided diesel volatility are added, the financial case strengthens further. Terminal operators we work with model cost per container move rather than cost per truck, and on that metric the TE9L usually wins the comparison.
