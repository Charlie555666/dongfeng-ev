The TZ3Z electric dump truck is built for the limestone haul roads of Quang Ninh, where a 282 kWh EV truck moves aggregate from pit face to crusher without the fuel burn and exhaust heat of a diesel tipper. Why Quang Ninh Quarries Favor an Electric Dump Truck

Quang Ninh is the heart of Vietnam's limestone and cement belt, and quarry haul roads are short, steep and repetitive: load at the face, climb to the crusher, dump, descend empty. That profile is ideal for electric drive because regenerative braking recovers energy on the descent and the truck rarely needs long-range reserve. With electricity at $0.08 to $0.20 per kWh versus diesel at $0.90 to $1.20 per liter, the TZ3Z cuts the cost of every haul while removing exhaust heat and noise from a site that often sits close to villages and farms.

Limestone Haul Cycle and Productivity

A typical Quang Ninh limestone loop runs 5 to 15 km between face and crusher with a loaded climb and an empty descent. The TZ3Z completes many cycles per shift on one charge and recaptures a large share of climb energy on the way back down. Operators report steadier climb speeds and less brake wear than diesel tippers, which improves both safety on the grade and round-trip time across a busy quarry road that sees constant mixer and loader traffic.
