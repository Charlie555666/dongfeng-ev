Addis Ababa is extending light rail and rebuilding arterial roads through crowded neighborhoods. Diesel tippers thunder through the night shift, disturbing residents and adding fumes to already busy streets, and the complaints they generate can stall a permit and a project. An electric truck hauls spoil almost silently and can work the low-traffic window without complaints. Across the Ethiopia electric truck market, construction fleets are the first to feel the noise-and-emission pressure that pushes them toward EVs, and the TZ3Z is built exactly for that constraint.

Beyond the neighbourhood benefit, quiet night hauling keeps the project on schedule. A crew that can work the 22:00-05:00 window doubles its effective day without fighting commuter traffic, which is often the difference between a rail segment opening on time or slipping a season.

The TZ3Z's sealed electric drivetrain lets contractors run the 22:00-05:00 shift through residential areas where a diesel tipper would draw noise complaints.
