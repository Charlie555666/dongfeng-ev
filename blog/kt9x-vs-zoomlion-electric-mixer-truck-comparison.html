KT9X vs Zoomlion: Electric Mixer Truck Comparison for Export Buyers

Ready-mix producers evaluating an EV truck for concrete delivery now face a clear choice between the Dongfeng KT9X and the Zoomlion electrical mixer. Both offer zero-tailpipe operation on the job site, but they differ sharply in drum drive architecture, battery layout, and after-sales footprint across the Middle East. This comparison gives procurement teams the hard numbers needed to specify the right electric truck for a 30 to 60 cubic metre per day batch plant.

Drum Capacity and Chassis Layout

The KT9X is a 6x4 mixer rated for 8 cubic metre drum with a 350 kW LvKong motor and a 362 kWh CATL LFP pack. The Zoomlion competitor uses a 7 cubic metre drum on a 6x4 chassis with a 315 kW motor and a 350 kWh pack. The KT9X places the battery low between the frame rails, which lowers the centre of gravity and keeps the drum balance stable on rough site access roads common in Iraq.

Drum Drive and Energy Use

The KT9X drives the drum through a dedicated 40 kW electric auxiliary motor powered from the main pack, so the drum spins even when the chassis is stationary on the pour point. The Zoomlion uses a belt-driven PTO-style electric motor that draws from the traction battery. On a typical 18 trip per day schedule the KT9X consumes 88 kWh per loaded delivery including drum rotation, versus 96 kWh for the Zoomlion because of higher aux losses.

Charging on the Batch Plant Site

A 240 kW DC pedestal at the plant recharges the KT9X from 20 to 80 percent in 54 minutes during the lunch and re-load window. We recommend two charge points per four-truck rotation so no mixer sits idle waiting for energy. For sites without three-phase supply, a 120 kW mobile charger on a diesel generator can bridge the gap during the first six months of operation.

After-Sales and Spare Parts

Shaanxi Fenghan Trading stocks KT9X drive units, reduction gears, and drum bearings at the Dubai warehouse, giving Iraq buyers a 7 to 10 day parts lead time. Zoomlion supports the region through a single JV partner with a 21 to 30 day lead time for imported drum components. For a batch plant where every idle mixer costs $400 per day in lost pours, the local parts buffer is a decisive factor.

  • Choose KT9X for larger 8 m3 drum and faster local parts support.
  • Choose Zoomlion if your existing fleet is already Zoomlion and you value single-brand service.
  • Specify an on-site 240 kW charger for either brand to protect the midday reload window.
  • Plan for 6 percent extra consumption in summer 45 deg C heat with cab air conditioning.

Five-Year Operating Cost

At $0.10 per kWh site electricity and 50,000 km per year, the KT9X spends about $11,000 annually on energy versus $12,500 for the Zoomlion. Maintenance runs 30 percent below diesel, saving roughly $7,000 per year. Over five years the KT9X's lower energy and parts cost totals about $92,000 in savings versus the Zoomlion equivalent, before counting the avoided diesel exhaust compliance upgrades at the plant gate.
