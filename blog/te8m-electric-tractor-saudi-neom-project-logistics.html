TE8M Electric Tractor for Saudi NEOM Project Logistics

Saudi Arabia's NEOM project is the largest greenfield build on the planet, and its logistics teams are under mandate to slash diesel use under Vision 2030, which makes a 400 kWh electric prime mover a strategic asset rather than a novelty. The TE8M electric tractor pairs a 400 kWh CATL LFP battery with a 510 kW LvKong motor, giving an EV truck that hauls 40-tonne project loads across the Tabuk-to-NEOM corridor on a single charge in 50 C heat.

Why NEOM logistics demand electric tractors

NEOM's contractors move steel, pipe and precast across distances of 150-250 km between the Tabuk railhead, the coastal construction zones and the mountain sites. Those are fixed, repetitive routes with depots at both ends - the ideal profile for battery-electric tractors. Diesel prime movers on this terrain burn 0.50-0.55 L/km under 40-tonne load, and at Saudi diesel prices near $0.95/L that is $0.50/km in fuel. The TE8M cuts that to roughly $0.18/km at depot electricity of $0.10/kWh and 1.5-1.6 kWh/km loaded.

Beyond cost, NEOM's sustainability scorecards now penalise diesel emissions on awarded packages. A zero-tailpipe tractor helps contractors hit the carbon targets written into their build contracts without buying expensive offsets.

Maintenance savings: what 35-40% really means

An electric tractor removes the diesel engine's oil, filters, injectors, turbo, EGR and exhaust after-treatment - the parts that drive most unscheduled breakdowns on 400 km desert hauls. Our fleet data shows EV trucks cut scheduled maintenance by 35-40% versus a diesel equivalent, which on a tractor running 250 days a year is roughly $4,500 in saved workshop bills. The LvKong motor's sealed drivetrain also tolerates the fine red sand that clogs diesel air filters, so NEOM site reliability improves rather than degrades in dust.

  • No engine oil changes: save about $1,400 per year in oil and filters.
  • No injector or turbo service: save about $1,200 per year.
  • Brake wear cut by regenerative braking on loaded descents: save about $1,000 per year.
  • Longer tyre life from smooth electric torque: save about $900 per year.
