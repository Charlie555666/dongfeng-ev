Iraq's Reconstruction Needs Heavy Haul

Reconstruction logistics means moving steel, cement, generators, and prefabricated modules along corridors that are improving but still rough. A prime mover must pull heavy loads in heat that exceeds 45°C in summer, on grades that punish weak powertrains. The TE8P is engineered for exactly this: a 120-ton GCW rating backed by a 510 kW LvKong motor.

Diesel prime movers on this duty burn fuel at a rate that makes every long corridor expensive, and their exhaust after-treatment struggles in sustained high-load, high-heat operation. The electric driveline removes those failure points while delivering torque instantly from a standstill.

Heat Management on Long Corridors

When a tractor pulls 120 tons up a grade in 45°C heat, both motor and pack generate serious thermal load. The TE8P's liquid-cooled loop holds the pack and inverter in their safe window, and LFP's high-temperature tolerance protects the 8-year / 4,500-cycle warranty where cheaper cells would swell and fail.

  • Active liquid cooling sustains full power on long climbs.
  • LFP chemistry tolerates heat and partial cycling better than NMC.
  • Regenerative braking on descents with heavy loads returns substantial energy.
  • Centralized pack monitoring supports warranty tracking per vehicle.
  • No diesel exhaust after-treatment to clog under sustained load.
