TE8P Electric Tractor for Saudi Rail Feeder Heavy Haul

Saudi Arabia's rail expansion - the North-South line, the Landbridge program and the freight terminals rising around Riyadh and Dammam - is reshaping how the Kingdom moves heavy cargo. But rail reaches only where rails go: every oversized module, transformer and project cargo piece still completes its journey by road, feeding the terminals from factories and sites 50-300 km away. That heavy-haul feeder duty, at 90-120 t gross combination weight, is exactly what the Dongfeng TE8P heavy-haul electric tractor is built for. This guide covers the Saudi case.

The Rail Feeder Duty Profile

Heavy feeder work runs fixed patterns: fabrication yard or port to rail freight terminal, loaded with project cargo, returning empty or with backhaul. Distances stay within 50-300 km per day - one comfortable charge for the TE8P's 600 kWh CATL LFP pack even at maximum combination weight. The duty's defining feature is the loading and discharge time at both ends: hours of crane work during which a diesel tractor idles or sits, and during which the electric truck charges. Heavy-haul scheduling, it turns out, contains the charging windows by design.

Measured energy consumption at 100-120 t GCW on Saudi highway profiles runs 2.6-3.2 kWh/km loaded and 1.3-1.5 empty. The 600 kWh pack provides the headroom that heavy-haul margins demand - this is not duty to run at the pack's edge, and the pack sizing reflects that.

Terminal and Site Charging

The charging plan follows the cargo: a 360 kW dual-gun unit at the fabrication yard or port base, and access at the rail terminal during discharge - terminals building for Saudi's freight expansion increasingly include heavy-vehicle power in their utility design. The operational rule for heavy haul is schedule-first: moves are permitted and planned days ahead, so charging slots book into the move plan rather than competing with it. Overnight full charges at base set every morning's baseline.

  • Book the terminal charging window with the discharge crane slot - they share the same hours.
  • Model every move at summer consumption plus 10%; heavy-haul margins are earned in planning.
  • Set full regen on any descent sections; at 120 t the brake wear saving is the largest in the fleet.
  • Track kWh per tonne-km by move; the data catches route, tyre and rigging issues early.

Heat and Desert Running at Weight

Saudi summer adds thermal load to an already extreme duty: 45 C ambient, hot road surfaces, and sustained high power draw at combination weight. The TE8P's liquid-cooled battery, motor and inverter hold output through it - the engineering difference that matters at 120 t, where any power derating stops being an inconvenience and becomes a safety case. Plan summer range at 280-310 km at full weight rather than 340; schedule the heaviest moves for morning where permits allow; and treat tyre management at these weights as the safety-critical discipline it is.

Project Economics
