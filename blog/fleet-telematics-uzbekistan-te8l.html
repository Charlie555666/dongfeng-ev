Privacy, Drivers and the Trust Framework

Driver data is the sensitive layer, and handling it honestly decides whether the telematics program runs on trust or resistance. The working principles: collect what the operation needs - energy, safety events, location on duty - and say plainly what is not collected; publish the policy, not just the consent form; use the data for coaching before discipline, and let drivers see their own numbers first. The TE8L's platform supports role-based access - drivers see their own performance, supervisors see the fleet - which enforces the policy technically rather than by promise. Fleets in Uzbek markets that run the transparent version report the cultural shift inside a quarter: drivers start checking their own energy scores, and the coaching conversation becomes a competition. The surveillance version produces the opposite: muted compliance and quiet resentment. The technology supports either; the policy chooses.
