Diesel sweepers rumble through Marrakech and Fes at 4 a.m., but a battery-electric unit runs near silently, so cities can extend cleaning hours without noise complaints. The KT1D pairs a 282 kWh pack with a 200 to 240 km range, enough for a full night of sweeping loops on a single charge. For an electric truck in a tourist city, quiet operation is not a comfort feature, it is the reason the contract is awarded.

The Morocco electric truck market is prioritizing air quality in visitation zones, and municipal tenders now score zero-emission vehicles higher than price alone. A Dongfeng EV truck that documents zero tailpipe output and low noise meets both the environmental and the social criteria buyers weigh when they award street-cleaning concessions.

How to Write the Municipal Tender

A good tender removes ambiguity so bidders price the electric truck fairly. The list below covers the clauses Moroccan cities use to get a compliant sweeper.

  • State the zero-emission requirement explicitly for all tourist-zone routes.
  • Set a maximum noise limit at sweep speed, measured at 7 meters.
  • Require an 8-year or 4,500-cycle battery warranty with transfer.
  • Ask for local service and spare-pack availability within Morocco.
  • Demand a documented charge plan for the 282 kWh pack overnight.

Cities should also require a demonstration week. A Dongfeng EV truck running real Marrakech lanes proves the range and noise claims before the contract is signed, and it gives cleaning crews time to learn the simplified EV controls that replace a diesel clutch and gearbox with a single pedal and a mode switch.

Which Charging Setup Suits a Depot

Most municipalities charge the KT1D at the depot between 10 p.m. and 5 a.m. on a 120 kW to 240 kW connection, which fills the 282 kWh pack well inside the window. Because the sweeper returns to base each morning, fast charging is rarely needed, and the lower charge rate also extends pack life. Cities that run two shifts can add one swap pack instead of a second charger.

The Morocco electric truck market is adding public depot chargers in tourist hubs, which gives a fallback if the municipal feed is limited. Plan the incoming service early, because upgrading a heritage-district depot connection can take longer than delivering the electric truck itself.

Cost and Resale for the City

A KT1D costs more upfront than a diesel sweeper, but removes diesel fuel and cuts maintenance on the electric drive. Over a seven-year concession the per-km cost favors the EV, and the 8-year warranty means the battery is covered for the full contract. At resale, a documented warranty transfer lifts the value of the used electric truck when the city refreshes its fleet.

Moroccan tourism boards increasingly co-fund clean vehicles because they protect the visitor experience. A municipality that bundles the KT1D into a wider green-city program can sometimes access subsidy alongside the purchase, improving the payback of the electric truck further.

Route Planning for Night Sweeping

A tourism city sweeper lives or dies by its route plan. Map the pedestrian and coach corridors first, then build loops that the KT1D can complete on a single 282 kWh charge before the first tourists arrive. Because the pack delivers 200 to 240 km of range and the sweeper draws lightly, a full night of lanes fits comfortably, and a 5 to 6 minute battery swap covers any special event that extends the shift. Plan the depot return so the truck is plugged in during the quiet midday window, keeping the pack topped without ever racing a charging curve.

Maintenance Differences From Diesel

An electric sweeper drops the engine oil, the diesel filter, the exhaust after-treatment and most of the transmission service from the schedule. The KT1D's maintenance centers on the brushes, the suction fan bearings, the brakes that regen rarely touches, and the 282 kWh CATL LFP pack's thermal and isolation checks. Over a seven-year concession that shift cuts workshop hours sharply, which matters for a municipal budget. The 8-year or 4,500-cycle warranty covers the pack, so the only large scheduled item is routine inspection rather than component replacement.

Training Municipal Crews

Crews trained on diesel sweepers need a short conversion, not a new career. The KT1D replaces clutch and gearbox with a single pedal and a mode switch, so a competent operator is safe within a day of supervised practice. Teach the high-voltage isolation routine, the quiet-operation habit so crews notice pedestrians, and the simple charge plan for the 282 kWh pack. Because the KT1D electric sweeper truck is near silent, add a short module on pedestrian awareness, which is the one new skill diesel crews must truly absorb.
