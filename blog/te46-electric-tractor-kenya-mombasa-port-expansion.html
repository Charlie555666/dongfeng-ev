Why Mombasa port needs electric terminal tractors

Mombasa handles more than 1.4 million twenty-foot containers a year, moving most of them on terminal tractors between the quay, the stacking yard and the inland container depot in short 1 to 3 km shuttles that run day and night. A diesel terminal tractor burns 8 to 12 litres per hour of idling shuttle duty, costing $9 to $14 per hour at Kenya's diesel price near $1.15 per litre, and its exhaust collects in the low-lying port basin. The TE46 removes that line with electricity at $0.09 to $0.18 per kWh and adds a 6-minute battery swap that beats any charger queue. For a port running 40 tractors, the fuel saving can exceed $1 million a year.

The second reason is throughput. A diesel tractor waiting at the fuel bowser is a tractor not moving boxes; the TE46's swap model keeps every unit on the ground through the 24-hour shift.

Duty cycle: a 24-hour port shuttle

A TE46 runs three 8-hour operator shifts covering 60 to 100 km per shift in stop-start shuttle. It draws about 1.6 to 2.2 kWh per km laden with a 40-foot box, so a 24-hour day uses 380 to 660 kWh across the pack and swap reserves. On the swap model, a depleted pack is exchanged in 6 minutes at the swap station, which recharges it slowly off-peak at the cheapest tariff. On the charge model, a 45 to 90 minute 20-80 percent top-up during the operator meal break keeps the unit turning.

Regenerative braking on the constant quay-to-yard deceleration recovers 12 to 18 percent per shift, and the instant electric torque handles the 65 t GCW start from standstill better than a diesel torque dip.

Cost per container move versus diesel

The port metric is cost per box move. For the TE46 at 1.9 kWh per km and a 2 km average shuttle:

  • Energy per move: 3.8 kWh x $0.12 per kWh = $0.46 per move.
  • Diesel equivalent: 10 L per hour / 8 moves per hour x $1.15 = $1.44 per move, three times higher.
  • Maintenance: no engine oil, no exhaust, regen brakes — about 35 percent lower per km.
  • Swap advantage: 6-minute swap vs 20-minute diesel refuel keeps more moves per shift.

Over a 1,000,000-move five-year life a single TE46 avoids roughly $0.9 million in fuel and $0.2 million in maintenance against a diesel unit, covering the FOB premium of $68,000 to $85,000 within 12 to 18 months of two-shift port operation.
