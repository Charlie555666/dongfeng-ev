Which Electric Truck Types Work Best on the Apron?

Airport ground support splits into three electric truck roles. Terminal tractors like the KT5L electric cargo truck handle baggage trains, cargo dollies and pushback-adjacent logistics - the highest-utilization role and the strongest electrification candidate. Electric cargo trucks in the 5-10 tonne class move freight between terminal, warehouse and aircraft. Specialist service trucks - sweepers, sprinklers and washing units - keep aprons, runways and stands clean and dust-controlled. All three share the duty profile electric drivetrains love: short distances, constant stop-start, long idle-free waits and nightly return to a fixed depot. How long does it take to charge an electric ground support truck?

On a 120 kW DC fast charger, an electric truck such as the TE46 charges from 20% to 80% in about 60-90 minutes, which fits into shift-change windows. Overnight slower charging at the GSE depot covers the base load at the lowest electricity tariff.

Are electric airport trucks really cheaper than diesel GSE?

What battery safety certification do airports require for electric trucks?

Airport safety departments generally require UN38.3 battery transport certification, an emergency response guide and documented high-voltage isolation design. Dongfeng EV trucks ship with CATL LFP packs carrying full UN38.3 test records plus first-responder documentation for airport fire services.

How many chargers does a small airport fleet need?

A starting fleet of five to fifteen electric trucks usually runs on two to four 60-120 kW DC chargers plus overnight charging points per parking bay. Exact charger count should follow a duty-cycle analysis of parked hours, which the truck supplier should support before ordering.
