Why UAE Municipalities Need an EV Truck

UAE cities are rolling out low-emission and pedestrian-priority zones where diesel plant is restricted. The KT7A meets those zones with no tailpipe and near-silent running, so crews can wash streets, medians and facades during the day without disturbing business districts. The same electric pack drives both the truck and the high-pressure pump, simplifying the powertrain and removing the separate engine a diesel washer needs just to run its pump. For a municipality reporting on air quality, the KT7A turns a historically dirty task into a clean one, and that is increasingly a contract requirement rather than a nice-to-have.

The KT7A is part of the established KT7A electric washing truck family, which shares tank, pump and chassis across Gulf municipal variants for easy spares planning and cross-emirate standardisation, so a single support chain can serve washers, sweepers and sprinklers from one parts catalogue.

Extreme Heat Operation in the Gulf

UAE summer ambient reaches 45-50C, the harshest condition for any battery vehicle. The KT7A uses active liquid thermal management that holds cells in the safe LFP window during both charging and the stop-start duty of a washing circuit. Depot charging is scheduled overnight when ambient is lowest, and the battery management system trims charge current if cell temperature drifts, protecting the 8-year / 4500-cycle warranty even under sustained Gulf heat. The pump motor is independently cooled so washing continues through midday, and the cabin is climate-rated for crew comfort during long summer circuits that a diesel truck would make unbearable.

  • Active liquid cooling protects cells at 45-50C ambient.
  • Overnight depot charging avoids peak heat and peak tariff.
  • Independent pump cooling sustains pressure in midday heat.
  • LFP chemistry avoids thermal runaway in sealed municipal compounds.
  • Low noise lets the truck wash near hospitals, schools and offices.
