What Is the FOB Price by Truck Type?

FOB (Free On Board) is the price of the truck loaded onto the vessel at a Chinese port, usually Shanghai or Tianjin. It includes the truck, export packaging, pre-delivery inspection and Chinese export documentation. How Much Are Freight, Duty and Registration?

Ocean freight for a single truck on a ro-ro vessel runs $3,000-5,500 to West Africa, $2,500-4,500 to the Gulf, and $5,000-9,000 to Latin America, depending on deck space and fuel surcharges. Container shipping of CKD kits costs less per unit but adds assembly cost at destination. Marine insurance adds roughly 0.4% of cargo value.

Import duty is where countries diverge. Gulf states charge 5% customs duty on trucks; many African markets charge 10-25% plus VAT, though EVs increasingly get relief - several governments now zero-rate duty on electric commercial vehicles or cut it to 5-10%. Registration, port handling and inland delivery typically add $1,500-4,000 per unit. In the Kenya electric truck market, for example, electric trucks benefit from excise relief that materially narrows the landed-cost gap against diesel.

What Documents Do You Need to Import an Electric Truck?

  • Commercial invoice and packing list issued by the exporter.
  • Bill of lading (ro-ro) or sea waybill naming the consignee.
  • Certificate of origin - China-issued, sometimes legalized by your embassy.
  • Export inspection certificate and factory test report for the vehicle.
  • Battery UN38.3 test summary and MSDS, required by carriers for lithium cargo.
  • Homologation or conformity papers for your market (GCC, SONCAP, INMETRO or national equivalent).

Hidden Costs Buyers Miss

Four items regularly surprise first-time importers. First, destination port demurrage if documents are late - budget $100-200 per day of avoidable delay. Second, charger procurement: a 120-240 kW DC depot charger costs $18,000-45,000 installed, and you need it before the trucks arrive. Third, driver and technician training, which Shaanxi Fenghan Trading bundles with fleet orders but costs real money if sourced separately. Fourth, spare parts stocking: a sensible two-year fast-moving parts kit adds 2-3% of fleet value.

How Payment Terms and Financing Work

Export payment for electric trucks follows standard capital-goods practice: 30% deposit with the order, 60% against shipping documents, 10% after arrival inspection - with irrevocable letters of credit the norm on first transactions and open terms developing with repeat business. Production lead time runs 45-75 days depending on specification and factory queue, which is why fleet buyers lock production slots a quarter ahead of their deployment date. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest electric truck to import from China?

The KT5J light electric cargo truck is the entry point at $52,000-62,000 FOB with a 310 kWh LFP battery, 18 t GVW and roughly 250 km of city range, landing near $60,000-70,000 in most African and Gulf markets.

How much is import duty on an electric truck in Africa?

Import duty on electric trucks in Africa runs 0-25% by country, with a growing list of markets cutting EV duty to 0-10%; always confirm the current HS 8704 EV tariff line with your clearing agent before ordering.

How long does shipping an electric truck from China take?

Shipping an electric truck from China takes 18-30 days to Gulf ports, 25-40 days to West and East Africa, and 30-45 days to Latin America by ro-ro vessel, plus 7-14 days of export preparation before departure.

Do I need special documents for the lithium battery?

Yes - carriers require a UN38.3 test summary and MSDS for the lithium battery, which the exporter supplies; without them the shipping line will refuse the booking even if the truck itself is cleared.

Can I get a discount on a fleet order of electric trucks?

Yes - orders of 10 or more units typically receive 5-8% FOB discounts from the factory, plus consolidated freight savings of $500-1,000 per truck on ro-ro bookings.
