Why a 350 kWh Mixer Pack

A mixer truck spends its energy on three loads: driving to site, turning the drum, and the return trip empty. The TZ8J's 350 kWh pack is sized for this stop-start urban cycle, with the drum driven electrically so it keeps turning even when the truck is stationary at a red light or a site gate. CATL LFP chemistry supports the 8-year or 4,500-cycle battery warranty and handles the repeated charge-discharge of a daily delivery rotation.

The LvKong motor in the TZ8J is rated in the 282-510 kW range, giving the torque needed to climb ramp approaches to metro viaducts while carrying a full drum. Electric torque from zero rpm also smooths pull-away from a standing start at a congested intersection, which reduces the spill and surge that diesel mixers show when the engine is asked to both drive and turn the drum.

Batching-Plant Charging Rotation

The practical model is a plant-side charging rotation. While one truck is loaded and dispatched, a returned truck plugs in at the batching plant between runs. Because deliveries cycle every 30 to 60 minutes near a city plant, a fast top-up between loads keeps the fleet within pack limits. For high-volume sites, a 5 to 6 minute battery swap rig maintains continuous output without a charging queue, which is critical when a metro segment pours on a strict schedule.
