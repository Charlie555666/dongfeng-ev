Why Coastal Beach Towns Need a Quiet Electric Truck

The KT3E electric garbage truck is proving to be the right electric truck for coastal municipalities in the Dominican Republic, where narrow beach roads and early-morning collection windows make noise and emissions a daily concern. Beach towns such as Boca Chica, Juan Dolio and Samana rely on tourism, and a silent EV truck that collects waste before the first guests reach the sand protects both the visitor experience and local air quality. For fleet managers comparing options, the KT3E combines a sealed compactor body with a zero-tailpipe powertrain, delivering the same 8 to 12 cubic meter payload as a comparable diesel unit without the idling fumes that drift into hotel courtyards.

Coastal collection is different from inland hauling. Routes are short, dense and repeated two or three times per day, which is exactly where an electric truck wins. Every stop is a chance for regenerative braking to recover energy, and the lack of engine noise means collection can start at 4 a.m. without a single complaint call to the mayor's office. The Dominican Republic has committed to cutting transport emissions in its tourism corridors, and municipal fleets are among the most visible places to show that progress.

Battery, Range and Charging on Coastal Routes

Coastal collection routes in the Dominican Republic are short and dense. A typical beach-town circuit covers 60 to 90 km per shift with frequent stops, which is ideal for an electric truck because regenerative braking recovers energy at every stop. The KT3E carries a 282 kWh CATL LFP pack that delivers 220 km of real-world range, comfortably covering two full shifts on a single overnight charge at the depot. For operators who cannot wait for a full charge, the optional battery swap reduces downtime to 5 to 6 minutes, matching a diesel refill.

Key operational facts for planners:

  • Depot charging uses a 120 kW DC station; a full 282 kWh charge takes about 2.5 hours overnight.
  • Regenerative braking recovers up to 15 percent of consumed energy on stop-start routes.
  • The LvKong 282 kW motor provides strong low-end torque for climbing coastal hills with a full compactor.
  • A 360 kW DC fast charger can top up 80 percent in under 50 minutes during midday peaks.
  • Battery thermal management keeps cells in the safe band between 15 deg C and 45 deg C.
