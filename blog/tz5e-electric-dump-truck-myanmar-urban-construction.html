TZ5E Electric Dump Truck for Myanmar's Urban Construction Projects

Why an Electric Tipper Fits Urban Construction

The TZ5E 6x4 electric dump truck is a strong electric truck choice for Myanmar's city construction where sites sit close to residential areas and noise curfews are tightening. With a 400 kWh CATL LFP pack and a 400 kW motor it hauls 20 to 28 t of spoil across 240 to 300 km of range per charge, covering multiple Yangon site loops before returning to depot.

Urban construction in Yangon runs against strict daytime noise limits, and a silent electric tipper can work the early and late windows that a clattering diesel loses. The TZ5E's near-zero site noise also improves relations with nearby residents and reduces the risk of complaint-driven stoppages.

Noise Rules and Night Work

Yangon's construction noise ordinance limits diesel plant after 22:00 near homes. The TZ5E's electric drivetrain lets it tip and shuttle with minimal sound, so contractors can extend productive hours into the early evening without breaching limits. The hydraulic pump is the main remaining noise source and runs only during lift.

Safety and Operator Comfort

The TZ5E emits no exhaust on site, so crews working in trenches and near the body are not breathing diesel fumes. Cabin preconditioning on grid power keeps the driver cool in Myanmar's 35 °C hot season without idling, and the low center of gravity from the floor-mounted pack improves tip stability.

Body and Liner Options

The TZ5E's 12 to 16 m3 V-shaped body suits general spoil, sand, and demolition waste, and buyers can spec a hardened liner for sharp broken concrete or a lighter alloy floor for soil. The hydraulic tip is inverter-driven off the main pack, so it lifts quietly and without a separate PTO, and the 400 kWh capacity covers a full shift even with frequent lifts.

Site Safety and Visibility

Yangon sites near schools and markets need a truck that does not blanket the neighborhood in diesel haze. The TZ5E's zero exhaust and low noise let it work the 07:00 to 09:00 window that a diesel loses to complaint, and the LED work lights and 360 camera package improve night safety on unlit plots.

Battery and Thermal Management

The 400 kWh LFP pack runs a liquid loop that shares heat with the motor and inverter, so on a 35 °C Yangon day the cells stay in the 25 to 35 °C window without wasting range on cooling. The 8-year warranty covers the tropical cycle, and Fenghan pre-conditions the pack on grid power so the first morning trip leaves with optimal cell temperature.

Multi-Site Rotation

Contractors with several Yangon sites rotate the TZ5E between them on a daily plan, since one truck covers 8 to 10 trips. A second unit lets the fleet cover a distant Hlaing Tharyar site while the first serves the city core, and both charge overnight at the central depot on a single 240 kW charger with staggered plug-in.

Operator Training

Drivers moving from diesel learn one-pedal control and the regen descent habit during a one-day Fenghan course. The instant torque needs a lighter right foot on the climb, and the trainer emphasizes charge-window discipline so the truck never drops below 15 percent mid-shift, which protects both range and battery health.

Resale and Residual Value

The TZ5E's LFP pack holds capacity well past the 8-year mark, so a unit rotated at 4 years typically retains 45 to 55 percent of FOB on the regional used market. That residual strength supports the finance case for a contractor adding a second unit, and Fenghan keeps the inspection record that proves battery health at trade-in.

Comparing against a Diesel on the Same Site

On a like-for-like Yangon inner loop, the TZ5E and a 6x4 diesel tipper carry the same 24 t and run the same trips, but the electric truck returns to depot with a full charge log and no fuel receipt to reconcile. The site manager gets a single energy number per km instead of a fuel card maze, which simplifies the cost report the head office requests each month.
