Why Tanjung Priok Favors an Electric Terminal Tractor

Tanjung Priok is Indonesia's busiest port, and terminal tractors run almost nonstop moving containers across short yard legs. A diesel yard horse idles constantly between moves, burning fuel and pumping exhaust into a confined terminal. The TE46 runs those same short, repetitive cycles on battery power and recaptures energy on every slowdown. With electricity at $0.08 to $0.20 per kWh versus diesel at $0.90 to $1.20 per liter, the per-kilometer energy saving is large on a duty profile that is almost all start-stop, and the quiet drivetrain improves the working environment for yard crews.
