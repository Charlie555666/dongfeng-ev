KT3F Electric Sprinkler Truck for Yangon Dust Control, Myanmar

The KT3F electric sprinkler truck is an EV truck designed for Yangon's dry season dust control, when the city's avenues, construction corridors and industrial roads turn to fine dust that chokes traffic and damages equipment. Why Yangon Needs an Electric Sprinkler Truck

Yangon's dry months from roughly November to April see dust blow from construction sites, unpaved lots and busy arterials, and municipal and private fleets run sprinkler trucks day and night to keep it down. A diesel sprinkler idles almost the entire shift while the pump runs, burning fuel for both propulsion and spraying, and adding exhaust to the very air it is supposed to clean. The KT3F's electric drivetrain powers chassis and pump from one battery with no idling burn and no tailpipe near residential streets.

The 262 kWh pack is sized for a full dust control circuit, and the chassis carries the tank, spray bar and hose reels needed for avenue and kerb work. For Myanmar fleet owners managing estate roads, ports and industrial parks, the KT3F is a low cost entry into electric trucks with a fast payback.

Yangon's dry season is the peak demand window, so the KT3F should be scheduled on the dustiest corridors at dawn and dusk when traffic is lighter and the spray is most effective.
