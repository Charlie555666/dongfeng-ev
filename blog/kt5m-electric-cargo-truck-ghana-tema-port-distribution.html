KT5M Electric Cargo Truck for Tema Port Distribution, Ghana

Ghana's import economy runs through Tema port, and from there goods flow inland to Accra and beyond on a congested but short distribution network. For fleet operators, the last-mile and mid-mile box truck is the workhorse that defines delivery cost. The KT5M electric cargo truck answers that need with a 282 kWh CATL LFP pack, a 282 kW LvKong permanent magnet motor and about 250 km of real range per charge. Operators tracking the Ghana electric truck market will see the KT5M as the most practical first step into zero-emission distribution.

Why Tema to Accra suits an EV truck

The Tema-Accra corridor is roughly 25-30 km of mixed highway and city street, ideal for a battery electric truck because the daily mileage stays well inside a single charge. Distribution fleets run multiple drops per day, and the stop-start pattern feeds regeneration back into the pack at every traffic light. Replacing a diesel box truck with the KT5M removes in-cab heat and fumes, improves driver comfort in Ghana's warm climate and eliminates tailpipe emissions inside dense Accra markets where pedestrians and stalls sit close to the road.

Daily range and duty cycle

A typical Accra distribution day covers 150-220 km across many short hops with full loading at the depot and partial loads after each drop. At 1.1-1.4 kWh per km lightly loaded and up to 1.6 kWh per km when full, the 282 kWh pack returns about 250 km of practical range, enough for a full shift without a midday charge for most routes. The liquid-cooled battery holds the 25-35 C operating window in Ghana's heat, and the 8-year or 4,500-cycle warranty covers the vehicle through its entire commercial life.

Depot charging for distributors

  • Install one 120 kW DC charger at the Tema or Accra depot for overnight filling.
  • Charge from 20 to 80 percent in 60-90 minutes between the morning and afternoon runs.
  • Time charging to off-peak grid hours to cut energy cost to 0.08-0.12 USD per kWh.
  • Fit a simple cable management arm so drivers connect in under two minutes.
  • Track each truck's state of charge from a single fleet dashboard.

Climate and battery care in West Africa

Ghana's humidity and year-round warmth demand a robust pack, and the KT5M seals its CATL LFP battery to IP67 with active liquid cooling that keeps cells in the 25-35 C band. LFP chemistry is stable across a wide temperature range and tolerates deep daily cycling, which is why the pack carries an 8-year or 4,500-cycle warranty. Operators note that the missing diesel engine bay also keeps the cab cooler and quieter, reducing driver fatigue on long Accra gridlock stretches.

Cold chain and cargo flexibility

The KT5M box body is not only for dry goods. The 282 kWh pack leaves ample margin to power an electric refrigeration unit for cold-chain delivery of fish, dairy and vaccines, because the auxiliary load draws from the same LFP pack rather than a separate diesel burner. Operators report quieter, fume-free refrigeration at the market stall, which protects product and crew alike. The flat cargo floor and 18-20 cubic metre volume also suit furniture, electronics and e-commerce parcels, so a single KT5M model can serve several business lines and raise asset utilisation across the week.
