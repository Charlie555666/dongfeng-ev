Sweeping and Dust Suppression Systems

The KT1D uses dual side brushes with a central main broom and vacuum intake feeding a sealed 4,000 liter hopper, while a 1,200 liter tank mists ahead of the brushes to keep Doha's fine dust from re-suspending. Two-stage cyclone plus panel filtration means the expelled air meets the clean-air intent of the program rather than re-releasing particles at street level.

The electric drivetrain lets the sweeper work pre-dawn shifts in residential districts without waking residents, and operators report 40 to 60 lane-kilometers of sweeping per 6 to 8 hour shift depending on debris load and dump frequency.

Range, Charging, and Heat Management

On night duty the KT1D returns 240 to 280 kilometers per chassis charge, far beyond a full sweeping shift, so a single overnight charge at the depot covers daily operation. The liquid-cooled CATL LFP pack is rated for Gulf summer temperatures, and night deployment avoids the worst of the heat for both pack and crew. A 120 kW charger restores 20 to 80 percent in about 90 minutes between shifts.

  • Charge overnight at the municipal depot on a 120 kW unit.
  • Run pre-dawn shifts to protect the pack from peak heat.
  • Use misting continuously during Doha's dusty shamal season.
  • Schedule hopper dumps at fixed points on the night route.
  • Rotate two sweepers per district so coverage survives charging.
