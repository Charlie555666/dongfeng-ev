Dongfeng TZ5E 6x4 Electric Dump Truck: 400 kWh CATL LFP for Urban Construction and Aggregates

The Dongfeng TZ5E 6x4 pure electric dump truck is the mid-capacity tipper in our export line-up, designed for urban construction sites, municipal solid waste handling, and mid-size quarry or cement plant aggregate haulage where the TZ3V 8x4 would be over-specified. With a CATL 400 kWh LFP traction battery, dual 315 kW continuous / 510 kW peak motors, and a 25-tonne gross vehicle weight rating, the TZ5E is the electric equivalent of the most popular diesel 6x4 tipper in China and offers the same body volume at a fraction of the energy and service cost.

400 kWh CATL LFP Battery

The TZ5E is built around a 400 kWh CATL LFP pack operating at 618 V nominal. The pack uses the same cell-to-pack structural architecture as the larger TZ3V, with liquid cooling and an integrated battery management system (BMS) that balances cells within 2 percent state-of-charge tolerance over the entire service life. The system is warranted for 5 years or 300,000 km in off-road and construction applications, with the same thermal-stability and cycle-life advantages as the larger unit.

On a typical urban construction cycle (5 km loaded + 5 km empty return, frequent stops, low-to-medium gradient), operators report 150 to 200 km of real-world range on a full charge, easily covering a full 8-hour shift. Opportunity charging at the lunch break and end of shift can extend daily operating hours indefinitely.

Drivetrain and Performance

The TZ5E uses a permanent-magnet motor rated at 315 kW continuous and 510 kW peak, with 3,100 Nm peak torque. A single-reduction rear axle transmits power to the rear tandem, and a multi-speed automated transmission provides launch and grade control. Top speed is governed at 90 km/h, and gradeability at full GVW is 30 percent.

Regenerative braking on the TZ5E returns energy on every deceleration event, which in stop-and-go urban traffic significantly reduces brake wear and adds 5 to 8 percent to daily operating range.

Body and Chassis

The standard TZ5E body measures 6,200 mm x 2,300 mm x 1,400 mm (19.9 m³ capacity) and is built from T700 high-tensile steel with a HYVA 169-series front-end tipping cylinder. The chassis uses a parallel ladder frame rated for 25-tonne GVW with a front 7-tonne axle and a tandem 2 x 11-tonne rear axle group. Ground clearance is 280 mm unladen, suitable for unsurfaced construction access roads and urban kerbs.

Best-Fit Applications

The TZ5E is particularly well-suited to the following operating profiles:

  • Urban building construction in noise- and emission-restricted city centres, where the absence of engine sound allows night-time operation in residential areas.
  • Municipal waste and recycling collection on short-cycle routes that return to a depot for opportunity charging.
  • Aggregate and sand haulage from quarry or river-pit to batching plant or rail head, with distances typically under 50 km one-way.
  • Cement plant infeed where raw-mill and kiln operations need reliable 24-hour truck supply.
  • Industrial site clean-up in steel mills, power plants, and chemical facilities where diesel emissions are restricted.

Comparison vs Diesel 6x4 Tipper

For fleet operators comparing the TZ5E to a conventional diesel 6x4 dump truck, the most important economic differences are:

  • Energy cost per kilometre is 55 to 65 percent lower in markets with grid or captive renewable electricity
  • Annual routine service cost is 50 to 60 percent lower (no engine oil, no fuel filters, no DPF)
  • Operator comfort is dramatically higher due to the absence of engine vibration and noise (cab noise level below 65 dB at full load)
  • Resale value is currently stronger in markets with active EV policy or carbon-pricing mechanisms
