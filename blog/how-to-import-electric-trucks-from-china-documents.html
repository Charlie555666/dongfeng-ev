How to Import Electric Trucks from China: What Documents Do You Need?

Quick answer: Importing an electric truck from China requires 8 core documents: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, China automobile export license, UN38.3 battery test summary, battery MSDS, and destination-country homologation or conformity papers. A licensed exporter prepares the first five; the battery documents come from the factory; homologation depends on your market.

Every week we talk to fleet owners who have chosen their truck — a TZ3V electric dump truck for a quarry, or a KT5M box truck for city delivery — and then stall on the same question: what paperwork does this actually take? The answer is more straightforward than diesel truck imports in one respect (no engine emission tests at many ports) and more demanding in another (lithium battery rules). Here is the complete working checklist we use for shipments to more than 30 countries.

Why do lithium battery documents matter more than the truck papers?

Diesel trucks clear ports with invoice, B/L and origin papers. Electric trucks add a layer most first-time importers do not expect: the battery pack is legally a Class 9 dangerous good. Every CATL LFP pack in a Dongfeng electric truck ships with a UN38.3 test summary — proof the pack passed altitude, thermal, vibration, shock, short-circuit, impact, overcharge and forced-discharge tests — plus an MSDS covering handling and fire response. Shipping lines will not accept the booking without both, and some ports (notably in the Gulf and West Africa) re-verify them at discharge. We attach both to every shipment by default; if your forwarder says battery papers are "optional", change forwarder.

Which homologation certificate does your country require?

This is the only document that varies by destination, and it is where importers most often lose two to four weeks. Examples from markets we ship to regularly:

  • Nigeria: SONCAP certificate from the Standards Organisation of Nigeria, arranged pre-shipment. See our Nigeria EV truck market page for duty structure and port notes.
  • Saudi Arabia and GCC: SASO conformity certificate plus GCC-standard compliance for lighting and tires.
  • Kenya and East Africa: PVoC (Pre-Export Verification of Conformity) inspection before loading.
  • Chile and most of Latin America: no pre-shipment inspection, but the vehicle must meet local homologation for registration — we supply the technical file.
  • European Union: WVTA or national small-series approval; EV trucks also need UN R100 (electric powertrain safety) and UN R10 (EMC) evidence, both of which Dongfeng models carry.

How can you avoid the three most common document delays?

  • VIN mismatch: the VIN on the invoice must match the chassis plate and the B/L exactly — one wrong character means manual inspection. We photograph every VIN before loading and send you the set.
  • Missing battery papers: request UN38.3 and MSDS copies with your proforma invoice, not after the vessel sails.
  • Wrong HS code: electric goods vehicles classify under 8704 (8704.60 in many tariffs for electric-only drivetrains). A wrong code can change duty by 10+ points and flags your file for review.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the single most important document when importing an electric truck from China?

The commercial invoice. Customs value, duties and VAT are all calculated from it, and for EV trucks it must state the model, VIN, battery capacity in kWh and the correct HS code (8704 series). Any mismatch with the packing list or bill of lading triggers inspection delays.

Do electric truck batteries need special shipping documents?

Yes. Lithium-ion packs above 100 Wh are Class 9 dangerous goods, so carriers require a UN38.3 test summary and an MSDS. Dongfeng's CATL LFP packs ship with both from the factory; without them the shipping line will refuse the booking.

Which certificate of origin should I ask for?

Ask for the CCPIT-issued Certificate of Origin. If your country has a trade agreement with China — ASEAN under ACFTA or Pakistan under CPFTA, for example — request the preferential Form E or Form P, which can reduce import duty by 5-30 percentage points.

Does China require an export license for electric trucks?

Yes. Complete vehicles require a Chinese automobile export license, which only licensed exporters can obtain. Shaanxi Fenghan Trading holds this license and manages the full export declaration, so buyers never interact with Chinese customs directly.

How long does customs clearance take for an imported EV truck?

With a complete document set, 3-7 working days in most African, Middle Eastern and Latin American ports. The two usual delay causes — missing UN38.3/MSDS battery papers and VIN mismatches — are both eliminated by a pre-shipment document check.
