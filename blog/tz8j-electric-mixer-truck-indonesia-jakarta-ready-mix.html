TZ8J Electric Mixer Truck for Jakarta Ready-Mix Concrete Fleets

Why an Electric Mixer Fits Jakarta

The TZ8J electric mixer truck is a practical electric truck for Jakarta's ready-mix concrete market, where high-rise and infrastructure projects need tight delivery windows and sites sit inside a congested 30 km radius. With a 350 kWh CATL LFP pack and a 360 kW motor it turns an 8 m3 drum across 200 to 260 km of range per charge, covering a full shift of plant-to-site runs.

Jakarta's air quality rules and the construction sector's push for greener sites make a zero-emission mixer attractive to developers bidding prestige towers. The TZ8J also removes diesel fumes from the site, a real safety gain where crews work around the rotating drum.

Plant-to-Site Range and Rotation

Jakarta ready-mix plants sit on the city edge, with sites typically 8 to 25 km away. The TZ8J runs 10 to 14 deliveries per shift, each a short haul with the drum turning, then returns to plant to reload. The range comfortably covers the day because the drum inverter draws from the pack only while mixing, and regen on the return empty run tops the pack back up.

  • Depart plant 06:00 at 100 percent, 8 m3 load.
  • Site loop 1: 12 km, pour, return empty with regen, 24 km round.
  • 10 to 14 loops per shift within a 30 km radius.
  • Mid-shift top-up at plant on 240 kW DC if batches run long.

Drum and Mixing Control

The TZ8J's 8 m3 drum turns off the dedicated inverter whenever the truck is moving, and slows to a gentle agitate at the site, so concrete quality holds without wasting pack energy. The control panel sets slump and batch ID, and the drum direction reverses for washdown, all from the cab touchscreen with no separate engine to warm up before the first pour.

Site Safety and Washdown

Jakarta sites near homes and malls value the TZ8J's quiet drum and zero exhaust, which let it arrive before the 07:00 unlock without complaint. After the pour the automatic washdown reverses the drum and sprays the fins, keeping the unit within weight limits and ready for the next batch without a diesel-idle cleanup that would foul the site.

Battery Thermal and Plant Power

The 350 kWh LFP pack shares a liquid loop with the motor and drum inverter, so on a 33 °C Jakarta day the cells stay in the 25 to 35 °C window. The plant charger pre-conditions the pack during the 10-minute load, so the first site run leaves with optimal cell temperature and the full 200 to 260 km range regardless of the morning heat.
